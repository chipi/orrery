# Term fix rounds — lessons per language (hand to every fix reviewer)

Collected from the 2026-10-31 rounds (science, essays, programs). Each line is a mistake a
reviewer made or nearly made. Add to it after every round.

## All languages

- *Burn* takes the binding only when it means an engine firing (a manoeuvre). Burning fuel,
  combustion, "burns the runway", a burned manuscript, stars that "burn" — keep.
- "No chemical burn could reach …" means chemical combustion: keep it in every locale.
- A first-stage booster (Falcon 9, Super Heavy) is correctly "the first stage" (étage,
  ступень, први степен, Erststufe, eerste trap, مرحلة): keep, reason `variant: first stage`.
  Only strap-on side boosters need the booster binding.
- The plain verb "to orbit" (umkreist, gravitent, cirkelt, обращается, кружи, 도는, 周回,
  公转, 绕…运行, تدور) is correct: keep, reason `variant: <word>`.
- Do not rewrite a correct standard compound or idiom to force the binding; keep it with
  `variant: <word>`. Do not add words or clauses the English does not need.
- Keep markdown link brackets and targets exactly; never translate a link target.
- When the binding form is there but inflected or compounded so the stems miss it, write
  `stem gap: <word>`.
- Report grammar slips you notice outside the term in your reply; do not edit them.

## By language

- **nl:** the noun "brand" is a fire — an engine burn is "ontsteking" (the verb "de motor
  brandt" is fine). Propellant is "drijfstof" (de-word: "de drijfstof", "die"), not
  "drijfgas". Gravity assist is "zwaartekrachtassist" (the binding since the missions round; never
  "zwaartekrachtsslinger" in new edits).
  Docking is "koppeling", not "aanmeren" or "dokken".
- **de:** "Zündung" is the instant of ignition; for the duration use "Brennphase".
- **fr / it / es:** "propulseur d'appoint", "booster laterale" and "propulsor lateral" are
  strap-ons. A whole launcher (Atlas) is "lanceur / lanciatore / lanzador", and a first stage
  is "premier étage / primo stadio / primera etapa". es stage is "etapa", never "estadio".
- **fr:** "arrimer" and "amarrer" are both accepted for docking. "carburant" is fuel;
  propellant is "propergol".
- **ru / sr-Cyrl:** decline every replacement and make verbs agree. Serbian is Cyrillic,
  ekavian, never Russian forms; the operator confirmed the Serbian bindings, «уз помоћ
  гравитације» is accepted, and «повратак у атмосферу» and «прикључни прстен/адаптер» are
  correct.
- **hi:** sun-synchronous is सूर्य-तुल्यकालिक, not सूर्य-स्थैतिक.
- **hi:** अवरोहण is descent and अवतरण is landing: change अवतरण only where the English means
  descent.
- **hi:** an engine burn is दहन (the site's form, 221×); प्रज्वलित is fine as the verb.
- **ar:** Modern Standard Arabic; matching ignores vowel marks. Flyby is التحليق القريب
  (مرور قريب accepted); الاقتراب (approach) and عبور (transit) are wrong. Rendezvous is
  الالتقاء. Make verbs agree with the gender of the new noun.
- **zh-CN:** trajectory is 轨道 (轨迹 accepted for a plotted path); never 自由返回轨迹 or
  轨迹修正. Full-width punctuation ，：；.
- **ja:** delta-v is デルタV.
- **ko:** keep particles grammatical (받침). 스윙바이 is gravity assist; a flyby is 플라이바이.
  탈출 궤도, 궤도 수정 and 자유복귀 궤도 are standard compounds.
