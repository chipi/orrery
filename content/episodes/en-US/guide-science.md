  worked-out math, and with the missions that use them.

  The encyclopedia is organized into ten tabs across the top.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  Orbits. <break time="200ms"/>
  Maneuvers. <break time="200ms"/>

  <break time="500ms"/>
  Each tab opens to a list of sections — eighty-five sections in total.

  <break time="800ms"/>

  Click any section card. <break time="500ms"/>
  The full article opens with worked math rendered by KaTeX, hand-drawn SVG
  diagrams, and links to read further.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  Where a section explains a phenomenon you can see in action elsewhere in
  Orrery, a chip at the top of the article links straight to it — Hohmann
  transfer to the Fly screen, free-return to the Plan screen, vis-viva to
  the Explore screen with the orbital-velocity overlay turned on.

  <break time="900ms"/>

  Notice the small question-mark chips throughout the rest of the site.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  Each one is a hyperlink directly into a specific Science section.
  <break time="400ms"/>
  Whenever you see one and don't know the term, click it. <break time="400ms"/>
  The encyclopedia opens at the right page.

  <break time="800ms"/>

  Try the search. <break time="500ms"/>
  Press Cmd-K — or Ctrl-K on a Windows or Linux keyboard. <break time="400ms"/>
  A search dialog opens. <break time="400ms"/>
  Type anything. <break time="400ms"/>
  The full text of all eighty-five sections is searchable, and results jump
  you directly to the matching paragraph.

  <break time="900ms"/>

  Seventy-one diagrams are hand-drawn SVG. <break time="500ms"/>
  Each one was authored from a primary source — a textbook, a paper, a NASA
  technical memo — and the source files for the SVGs are committed to the
  same repository as the site itself. <break time="500ms"/>
  Click any diagram for the credit and the source.

  <break time="900ms"/>

  Every formula has a citation. <break time="400ms"/>
  Every constant has a reference. <break time="400ms"/>
  Every diagram has an author and a license. <break time="400ms"/>
  This isn't a wiki. <break time="500ms"/>
  It's a working reference written specifically to make the rest of Orrery
  understandable.

  <break time="800ms"/>
