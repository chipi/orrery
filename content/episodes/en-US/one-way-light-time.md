<speak>
  Hold a conversation. <break time="500ms"/>
  Notice how quickly you respond to what the other person says.
  <break time="400ms"/>
  The gap between their last word and your first is usually less than half a second.
  <break time="400ms"/>
  When it stretches past two seconds, you both feel it — the air goes flat.
  <break time="400ms"/>
  Beyond ten seconds, you've stopped talking. <break time="300ms"/>
  You're taking turns leaving messages.

  <break time="800ms"/>

  The radio signal between Earth and the Moon takes <emphasis level="moderate">one point three seconds</emphasis>,
  one way. <break time="500ms"/>
  Two point six seconds, round trip.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  Walter Cronkite's broadcasts during Apollo 11 had a noticeable pause every time Houston spoke
  to the crew. <break time="400ms"/> The astronauts spoke back. <break time="300ms"/> The pause held.
  <break time="400ms"/> The conversation worked — barely.

  <break time="800ms"/>

  Now imagine you tried to drive a Mars rover. <break time="500ms"/>
  One-way light time to Mars at average distance: <emphasis level="moderate">about fourteen minutes</emphasis>.
  <break time="400ms"/>
  Round trip: <emphasis level="moderate">about twenty-eight minutes</emphasis>.
  <break time="600ms"/>
  You push the joystick forward. <break time="300ms"/> Nothing happens for fourteen minutes.
  Then the rover starts moving. <break time="500ms"/>
  You see it moving fourteen minutes after that.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  By the time your "stop" command arrives, the rover has been driving — by your now-stale
  instructions — for half an hour.

  <break time="900ms"/>

  This is why <emphasis level="strong">there is no manual mode</emphasis> on the rover.
  <break time="400ms"/>
  There can't be.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  The rover has to think for itself in every short interval between the planning team's daily commands.
  <break time="500ms"/>
  Beyond a couple of light-seconds of distance, every spacecraft humanity has ever sent has had to
  become its own captain.

  <break time="800ms"/>

  The Moon is close. <break time="400ms"/>
  Mars is not.
</speak>
