# ADR-089 — Super-circular loft/skip model for lifting atmospheric entry

> Status · Accepted · 2026-08-31 · Operator-directed (Physics Lab spaceflight program) · builds on
> ADR-088 (range-control entry) + RFC-036 (2-DOF descent) · unblocks Mars guided-lifting entries
> and the lunar-return 11 km/s skip (#29 Phases 2–3)

**Gating sentence.** The descent integrator (`descent-physics.ts`) gains a **loft/skip capability**
for LIFTING super-circular entries: the 2-DOF γ integration is enabled when the vehicle has lift
(not only for sub-circular entries), the γ-floor that forbade climbing is lifted for lifting bodies
(so γ can go negative = loft), and an **energy-conserving exo-atmospheric coast** holds the specific
orbital energy above the atmosphere so explicit Euler doesn't drift the skip arc. `skip_out` and
`second_entry` beats mark the crossing. Non-lifting profiles are BYTE-IDENTICAL — none of the 44
shipped profiles has lift on a super-circular arrival, so all keep the validated fixed-γ path.

## Context

ADR-088 gave the entry integrator a downrange DOF + range-control bank, but capped γ at ~0.1°
descending (never fly upward) and disabled the 2-DOF integration entirely for super-circular
arrivals. That made two real cases unmodellable:
- **Lunar-return entry** (Apollo CM at ~11 km/s): the corridor is a knife-edge and the vehicle
  lofts back above the atmosphere (the Apollo-4 double-g-pulse) before its final descent.
- **Mars guided lifting entries** (Curiosity/MSL, Perseverance, Tianwen-1): super-circular
  interplanetary arrivals that flew real bank-steered lifting entries, modelled here as ballistic.

Measurement (ADR-088 Phase 1a) established that the γ-floor is irrelevant at LEO energy — Earth
capsules don't loft there — so lifting it is safe for the LEO capsules and only activates the loft
where the energy genuinely produces one (super-circular).

## Decision

1. **Enable 2-DOF for lifting super-circular.** `dynamicalGamma = (subCircular || hasLift) &&
   gammaIsDynamical(phase)`. Ballistic super-circular arrivals keep fixed-γ (unchanged).
2. **Lift the γ-floor for lifting bodies.** `if (!hasLift && γ < 0.0017) γ = 0.0017`. A lifting
   entry may climb (γ<0) — that IS the skip; a ballistic capsule still can't fly upward.
3. **Energy-conserving coast.** Above `SKIP_ALT_M` (105 km) the air is negligible and explicit
   Euler drifts specific energy over a long arc. On entering the coast we record the specific
   orbital energy `E = v²/2 − μ/r` and each step correct `v = √(2·(E + μ/r))` — gravity is
   conservative, so the coast keeps E exactly, bounding the drift. Drag resumes on re-entry.
4. **Skip beats.** `skip_out` when the capsule first crosses above `SKIP_ALT_M`; `second_entry`
   when it falls back below. (Fully hyperbolic exit — never returns — simply times out at `maxTS`
   as an honest mission failure; the too-shallow corridor edge.)

Validated behaviour (Apollo CM aero, 11 km/s, full lift-up): 4° too-shallow → skips to a ~40,000 km
ellipse and fails to return (knife-edge corridor); 5.5–6.5° corridor → modest skip (max alt ~160 km),
lands at **4–8 g** (real lunar return ~6.5–8 g); ≥7° or lift-down → digs in at 10–16 g. Tests in
`descent-physics.test.ts` lock the corridor skip, the lift-up-vs-lift-down g trade, and the
too-shallow skip-out failure.

## Consequences

- **Unblocks Phase 2 (Mars guided lifting) and Phase 3 (lunar-return + Apollo round-trip capstone)**
  — the model can now fly the super-circular lifting entries those need.
- **No behaviour change for the 44 shipped profiles** (none has lift on a super-circular arrival):
  descent + HUD suites green (268 → 25 new physics tests).
- **The LEO lifting capsules are unaffected** — they don't reach γ<0 (measured), so the lifted
  floor changes nothing there; their ~1–1.5 g-above-flown residual (ADR-088 Phase 1a) stands until
  a future active-g-limit controller, and is separate from this skip work.

## Non-goals / honest simplifications

- **Crossrange / bank reversals** — still out (planar model).
- **The coast is Keplerian-energy-held, not a full two-body propagation** — it conserves E but
  integrates the same planar EOM through the thin arc; fine for the teaching skip, not an ephemeris.
- **A fully hyperbolic (escaping) entry** is reported as a timeout failure, not a distinct
  "escaped" outcome — acceptable for the corridor lesson (too-shallow = lost).
- **Not re-calibrating the LEO capsules** — their peak-g residual is an active-g-limit-controller
  gap (ADR-088), not a loft gap.
