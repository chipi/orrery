# ADR-095 — Eccentric planets, the JPL reference and vector v∞ in the shared transfer model

> Status · Accepted · 2026-11-07 · Operator-directed ("align with the JPL as a reference … validate all the clients using that equation") · supersedes the "all planets use circular `a`" line of **ADR-028** §Schema additions · builds on **ADR-026** (multi-destination grid) and **ADR-085** §D2 (vector arrival v∞)

## Context

`destinationPos` (mission-arc) and `destinationHelioXY` (lambert-grid) place every body on the
mean-element model shared by `/fly`, the `/plan` porkchops (precomputed and worker), the Lab
porkchop and the moon porkchops. ADR-028 kept the planets circular so the short-way Lambert grid
converged and the old goldens held. Checked against the JPL Keplerian ephemeris
(`physics/ephemeris`, the one the sky views and the mission blueprints use), 1970–2035:

- circles put Mercury up to 24° from its real longitude, Mars 10.8°, the giants 5–6°, Earth 1.9°;
  `/fly` pins every labelled flyby waypoint to these positions, so flybys sat off the real geometry;
- the grids counted days from 2026-01-01 but placed planets with J2000 elements and no offset, so
  every grid planet but Earth was out of phase (Jupiter's window ~80 days early) while a hand-set
  Mars `L0` hid it for Mars and broke `/fly`'s Mars;
- the grids priced v∞ as if Earth and the target moved tangentially at circular speed — with
  eccentric planets Mars alone has up to ~2 km/s of radial motion.

## Decision

1. **The JPL ephemeris is the reference.** `planet-positions-jpl.test.ts` requires every planet
   and Earth within 0.6° of it, 1970–2035; `porkchop-windows.test.ts` requires each Venus / Mars /
   Jupiter porkchop window within weeks of the JPL Hohmann phase date, Δv near Hohmann.
   `scripts/fly/planet-model-check.ts`, `blueprint-fit-audit.ts` and `porkchop-window-check.ts`
   print the tables.
2. **Planets and Earth carry their eccentricity** (`e`, `ϖ` from `planets.json`):
   `buildPlanetDestination` and `EARTH_ORBIT`. Max error is now 0.47° (Mercury), Earth 0.02°.
   `marsPos` delegates to `destinationPos`. Bodies below the convergence concern of ADR-028
   (Ceres, Arrokoth) stay circular.
3. **Grid epoch.** `HELIO_GRID_EPOCH_SIM_DAY` (9497 = 2026-01-01 from J2000) is added before any
   grid places a body. Mars `L0` is JPL's J2000 value again.
4. **Lambert, prograde.** `solveLambertPrograde` picks the long way when the transfer angle
   exceeds 180° and falls back to the high branch, so slow inner-planet transfers solve.
5. **Vector v∞ at both ends.** `helio-state.ts`: `bodyHelioXY` (the shared position model),
   `bodyHelioVelXY` (velocity), `lambertEndVelocities` (Lagrange f/g vectors at r1, r2 from the
   solver's `p`). Both grids take `|v_transfer − v_body|` at departure and arrival.
6. **Drawn orbits follow the model.** `/fly` rings (3D and the 2D fallback) are traced from
   `orbitPath` / `earthOrbitPath`, so a body sits on its ring on every day.

## Consequences

- Porkchops and `/fly` paths were regenerated (`npm run precompute-porkchops`,
  `generate-fly-trajectory.ts` for every file in `static/data/fly-trajectories/`). Windows: Venus,
  Jupiter, Saturn within 1–10 days of the JPL phase dates; Mars 2026 minimum 2026-10-30 (NASA's
  handbook lowest-energy departures Oct 31 / Nov 14; it was 11-12).
- Tests that encoded circles changed: Ceres for the circular-ring case, radius bounds for Mars and
  Earth, the soundness check compares the arc end at `flyby_day`, the fast moon-transfer guard at
  1.4 yr (1.2 yr from day 280 now needs a hyperbola the elliptic solver does not produce; the
  guard still fails on a scalar v∞).
- The `/fly` HUD's transfer ellipse reaches the target's real radius at arrival; the render-state
  hook exposes it (`data-transfer-a-au`) for the e2e vis-viva check.
- Any change to the elements, the epoch or the solver re-runs the three checks above and
  regenerates the porkchops and `/fly` paths.
