> **Why this is a PRD.** It adds a public route built from an internal translation tool. It
> promises readers one word per term on every page, and the term gate in CI keeps that promise.

## 1 · What

`/science/glossary`: a page like the glossary at the back of a technical book. For every technical
term in the glossary's `term` section (20 at launch: orbit, trajectory, burn, flyby, orbit
insertion …), in the reader's language:

- the term, sorted alphabetically in that language;
- a one-sentence definition;
- **Read more:** the /science page that explains it (18 of 20; rendezvous and payload have none);
- **Used on:** the pages that use the term most (up to 5).

Each entry has an anchor (`/science/glossary#gravity-assist`) for deep links.

**Where it lives:** a "see also" tab of /science, next to Reading List and Watch List (operator,
2026-10-31). The terms are the science section's vocabulary: every definition but two points
to a science article, and the articles mostly explain these terms. Missions, fleet and essays
use them without explaining them, and the "used on" links show that use. Also linked from
the footer, the phone drawer and the end of the /science landing.

## 2 · Data path

- **Terms and forms:** `native-terms.json` `term` (binding form per locale; `_read_more` names the
  science page).
- **Definitions:** overlays `i18n-src/<locale>/glossary/terms.json`, translated like any overlay.
  They are checked by `problems()` and by the term gate, so the definitions use the binding forms.
  They live in their own i18n chunk (`glossary`), so no other page loads them.
- **Build:** `scripts/build-glossary.mjs` (in `npm run build`) writes
  `static/data/glossary/<locale>.json`, one file per locale. It resolves names, definitions, the
  read-more page and the "used on" pages with their titles. "Used on" is counted on the English
  text, the way `term-drift.mjs` finds a term. The output is generated and gitignored.
- **A term added** to the glossary (with its definitions) appears on the page; no code change.

## 3 · Guards

- `scripts/build-glossary.test.ts`:
  - every term has a definition in all 14 locales (no English fallback) and a title for every
    linked page;
  - the science, essay and program links exist;
  - the route mapping is tested.
- The term gate (`term-glossary.test.ts`) covers the definitions (`glossary` tree).
- `check-internal-links` (build) checks every rendered link, and the a11y gate includes `/science/glossary`.

## 4 · Relation to other pages

## 5 · Terms linked from the text (shipped 2026-11-04, 0.9.6)

In /science articles the first mention of a glossary term links to its entry
(`/science/glossary#gravity-assist`), closing the loop in both directions. Rules (operator,
2026-11-04: at most 2 per paragraph, a quiet link):

- **Where:** the 101 narrative and the body paragraphs, in reading order. Not the title, the
  lede, captions or formula captions. Essays, missions and fleet are not linked yet.
- **How often:** each term once per article, at its first mention; at most 2 term links per
  paragraph, and a term crowded out moves to its next mention.
- **Never:** on the term's own read-more article; inside an author's `[text](link)`; on pages
  where `_en_skip` gives the word another sense (the Shuttle "orbiter").
- **Which words:** a mention counts where the term gate would see it: the English of the same
  field uses the term, and the locale's text matches the glossary's stems (inflections, Korean
  particles, Arabic clitics included). In scripts with spaces the link covers the whole word, up
  to 4 letters beyond the stem (an ending, never a compound: de «Nutzlastspezialisten» is not
  linked as "payload"). The word must share a piece of the binding form (3 letters, 2 in ja /
  zh-CN / ko), so a plain word the gate accepts (ja 探査機 "probe" for orbiter) is not linked;
  a word that is more another term's (es «orbitador», ja 軌道 where the paragraph also says
  "trajectory") is skipped.
- **Look:** the word keeps the body colour with a dotted teal underline; solid on hover and
  focus.

**Data path:** `scripts/glossary-links.mjs` (called by `build-glossary.mjs`) writes
`static/data/glossary/<locale>.links.json`: per article, per paragraph, `[term id, offset in the
plain text, text]`. The article's load reads it; `parseInline(para, terms)` in
`src/lib/science-inline.ts` links a term only where its text still sits at that offset, so an
overlay edited after the build loses the link instead of getting a wrong one. About 30 KB per
locale, not precached; ~480–580 links per locale on 168 articles (median 3 per article).

**Guards:** `scripts/glossary-links.test.ts` (the rules, mutation-checked: lifting the
compound limit fails it), `src/lib/science-inline.test.ts` (offsets, author links, moved
text), `build-glossary` problems (every link's text sits at its offset; every term id exists),
`tests/e2e/science.spec.ts` (en, de, ar: a term link lands on its entry; once per article; not
on its own page).

## 5b · Later still: a small visual per term (operator idea, 2026-10-31)

After the term links, the final item. Each entry gets a small visual in the style of the
science pages' diagrams, because a picture helps with the concept: apogee/perigee on an
ellipse, a burn as a thrust arrow on an orbit, a gravity-assist bend, staging, the entry
corridor. It uses the house pipeline in `docs/guides/diagram-art-style.md` (precise SVG
sketch → Higgsfield `nano_banana_pro` → webp) so the glossary and /science read as one hand,
and it reuses a science page's diagram where one already shows the term. Generation spends
Higgsfield credits: count the terms, estimate the credits and get a go before running.

## 6 · Non-goals

- **Definitions for every science page.** Only the glossary's terms appear here.
- **Search on the page:** 20 entries; site search (Cmd-K) covers lookup. Revisit at ~100 terms,
  and add an A–Z jump bar then too.
- **Per-locale "used on" counts:** the pages are the same in every locale, so the counts come
  from the English text.
