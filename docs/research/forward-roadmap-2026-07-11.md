# Forward-Looking Space Programs Catalog — 2026-07-11 (comprehensive refresh)

> Refresh of `forward-roadmap-2026-06-09.md`, re-run 2026-07-11 from 6 parallel agency-scope research passes (China, Russia, ESA+JAXA, NASA+US-commercial, ISRO+newcomers, plus the June backlog). **Intent changed:** the June doc was a curation backlog for a *minimum representative cut* (24 missions). This doc is scoped for **per-agency completeness** — finalize each major program's forward roadmap, not just one flagship each. The June doc is kept for history; this one supersedes it as the working backlog.
>
> **Confidence legend:** `A` = officially announced + funded, hardware/schedule exists. `D` = in active development. `P` = proposed / study / paper. Anything past ~2030 is aspirational regardless of tier.
>
> **`→` column** maps each item to an orrery entry: `M` = mission (`static/data/missions/{body}/`), or a fleet category (`launcher`, `crewed-spacecraft`, `lander`, `rover`, `station`, `surface-base`, `orbiter`, `observatory`, `space-suit`, `cargo-spacecraft`, `constellation`, `launch-site`, `ground-observatory`).
