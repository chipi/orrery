## Why this UXS exists
The fly screen has the most complex layout of any screen — dual HUD panels, timeline scrubber, CAPCOM panels, and a full-viewport 3D or 2D canvas. This UXS defines the static appearance of all components and the visual distinction between outbound and return arcs.

## Principles
- The spacecraft is always visible and identifiable
- Past trajectory is solid; future is dashed — the distinction is immediate
- Outbound arc (blue) and return arc (violet) are distinguishable without a legend
- HUD panels are readable at a glance — data, not decoration
- CAPCOM mode adds information without hiding the canvas

## Scope
In scope: nav bar, 3D scene, 2D canvas, HUD panels (left identity, right navigation, right systems), CAPCOM panels, timeline scrubber, bottom bar, milestone markers (FLYBY, RETURN), spacecraft icon.
Non-goals: animation easing, CAPCOM panel slide-in timing.
Boundary note: CAPCOM event triggering logic belongs in the screen module.

## Layout

### HUD panels (2D and 3D)
```
LEFT (identity, 225px):         RIGHT-TOP (navigation, 228px):
┌─────────────────────┐         ┌──────────────────────┐
│ ▌ EARTH → MARS FLY  │         │ ▌ NAVIGATION          │
│   ORRERY-1          │         │   Heliocentric vel.   │
│   Falcon Heavy      │         │   Distance from Earth │
│   Date / MET        │         │   Distance from Mars  │
└─────────────────────┘         │   Signal delay / Sun  │
                                 └──────────────────────┘
                                 RIGHT-BOTTOM (systems, 228px):
                                 ┌──────────────────────┐
                                 │ ▌ SYSTEMS             │
                                 │   Propellant bar      │
                                 │   Δv remaining bar    │
                                 │   ETA / Phase         │
                                 └──────────────────────┘
```

Rows inside each panel are a label/value grid (UXD-043): a label column shared by the rows, the value left-aligned and wrapping inside its own column; the panels above the rows (title, phase pill, notes) span both columns. The same grid is the spacecraft info card's and the CAPCOM communications rows'.

### CAPCOM panels (left side, 222px, shown when CAPCOM active)
Three stacked panels replacing the left identity panel:
1. Mission events ticker (max-height 190px, scrollable)
2. Communications (signal delay, RTT, DSN contact)
3. Anomaly monitor (nominal / caution / critical)

### Timeline scrubber (bottom bar, 68px)
Play/pause button · speed controls (1d/s, 7d/s, 30d/s, 90d/s) · scrubber track · MET display

**Launch camera (UXD-046).** The launch is shot by a seeded camera director: an opening from a pool (long-lens tower clear, a pad-level wide, an overhead drop, a dusk wide), then shots by altitude band with a beat for Max-Q, each separation and the fairing, eased blends and hard cuts on the beats, the vehicle filling 40 to 70 % of the frame. The first seconds are a vertical rise (the body upright until its base clears the tower). The sky, sun and clouds follow the Sun at the pad at the real launch time when the mission has one, and are seeded for a Plan flight. Reduced motion: cuts only, each shot a held pose. `?cam=classic` keeps the old camera for one release; `data-director-shot` names the active shot.

**Launch pad (UXD-047).** The launch draws the pad of its launch site and year, in the tower's low-detail style and at its real height against the rocket: the Apollo Launch Umbilical Tower, the Shuttle Fixed and Rotating Service Structures, the Soyuz support arms that fall away at T+0, the Falcon strongback that leans back at liftoff, the Atlas V umbilical tower, and service towers for the Cape, Kourou, the Chinese sites, Tanegashima and Sriharikota. A site with no model keeps the generic tower. Each pad model is listed on /colophon.

**Marker row (UXD-042).** One row of markers on the track for the whole journey: the launch's flight-plan beats (liftoff, MAX-Q, stage sep, fairing, SECO, and the parking orbit, injection burn and spacecraft separation when the mission leaves orbit) in the ascent slice, the mission's milestones (gold dots) and flight-director stages (gold diamonds) in the cruise band, the descent beats in the descent tail where the mission has a profile (a plan flight has none). Beats are 2 px muted-blue ticks with a hover and focus label (`hudLabel` text and clock); a click seeks there; ticks closer than 10 px collapse into the first with a `+N`. The row is visible during the launch and the descent.

### Spacecraft icon
2D: white chevron (triangle with cut tail, 9px half-size) oriented along velocity vector, blue glow behind.
3D: cone (nose) + cylinder (body) + inverted cone (nozzle), oriented along velocity vector.

## Component states

**Arc (outbound, past):** solid blue 1.5px line
**2D canvas (UXD-043):** rings and anchors neutral white (0.45 and 0.7 alpha), stars 0.15 to 0.51 alpha, labels 11 px; the destination's identity colour is on its live body and label; the scale fits the destination or trajectory; only the active destination is drawn beside Earth (the host plus the named moon for a planetary-moon flight); every line clears 3:1 over `#04040c` except the faintest stars.
**Arc (outbound, future):** dashed blue 75% opacity (was 20%; 20% measured 1.2:1), [4,6] dash pattern
**Arc (return, past):** solid violet 1.5px
**Arc (return, future):** dashed violet 75%, [4,6]
**Flyby ring (near Mars, progress 0.45–0.55):** pulsing red ring, opacity 0.3–0.7
**Flyby ring (all other times):** hidden
**CAPCOM button (inactive):** `border var(--color-accent-strong)`, `bg var(--color-accent-soft)`, `color var(--color-state)`
**CAPCOM button (active):** `border var(--color-accent)`, `bg rgba(80,112,255,0.22)`, `color #fff`, `box-shadow 0 0 10px rgba(80,112,255,0.4)`
**Anomaly (nominal):** `border rgba(40,40,40,1)`, `color var(--color-state)`, text "ALL SYSTEMS NOMINAL"
**Anomaly (caution):** `border rgba(255,200,80,0.5)`, `color #ffc850`, text "⚠ CAUTION: FUEL LOW"
**Anomaly (critical):** `border rgba(255,80,80,0.6)`, `color #ff6060`, blinking, text "⚠ CRITICAL"

## Accessibility

## Extension — Flight params HUD readout (v0.1.7 / ADR-027)

When the loaded mission has a populated `flight` sub-object, /fly's HUD surfaces real per-mission flight parameters instead of the synthesised defaults.

### Layout addition

A new **FLIGHT PARAMS** group sits below the existing NAV HUD on the left rail:

```
LAUNCH C3        11.2 km²/s²
ARRIVAL V∞        5.6 km/s
TOTAL ∆v          6.1 km/s     [src: NASA MSL Press Kit]
```

Source citation collapses on phones (≤767 px) into a tap-to-reveal tooltip on the row.

### Sparse-data caveat banner

When `flight_data_quality !== "measured"`, a one-line banner sits above FLIGHT PARAMS, amber tint (`#ffc850` border, `rgba(255,200,80,0.18)` bg). Plain-language copy is i18n-keyed.

### Missing values

Render as `—` (em-dash). Never as `0` or `null`. Never invent a plausible-sounding default.

### Arc geometry hook

If `flight.arrival.v_infinity_km_s` is present, the outbound arc's terminal segment uses it (not the Lambert-default V∞) so two missions to the same destination on the same dates render visibly distinct paths when their target geometry differs. Falls back to the v0.1.6 Lambert geometry when absent.

### Tokens (extension)

**FLIGHT PARAMS group bg:** `rgba(20,30,55,0.72)` matching existing HUD tint
**FLIGHT PARAMS label:** Space Mono, 11 px, `rgba(180,200,255,0.85)`
**FLIGHT PARAMS value:** Space Mono, 13 px, accent per-row (∆v `#ffc850`, V∞ `var(--color-accent-hover)`, C3 `#4b9cd3`)
**Caveat banner:** `#ffc850` border 1 px, `rgba(255,200,80,0.18)` bg, 12 px Space Mono uppercase

### Acceptance criteria (extension)

- [ ] When `mission.flight` is present, FLIGHT PARAMS group renders below NAV
- [ ] When `mission.flight` is absent, no FLIGHT PARAMS group appears (no regression)
- [ ] When `flight_data_quality !== "measured"`, caveat banner shows
- [ ] Missing fields render as `—`, never as `0` or `undefined`
- [ ] Source citation accessible (visible desktop, tooltip mobile)
- [ ] Arc terminal uses real V∞ when present
