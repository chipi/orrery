# UXS-017 — The gallery: the hub, the deep-sky viewer, the note under the pages

> Related · IA §page-shell · Design System §4.6 (`.page-note`) and §5.21 (the viewer)

## Why this UXS exists

[UXS-000](UXS-000-orrery-ux-architecture.md) §Next listed "no spec yet for the deep-sky viewer": the gallery family (`/gallery`, `/gallery/deep-sky`, `/posters`, `/patches`, `/sourcing`) had no spec. This one holds the rules that the spec guards.

## 1 · The hub (`/gallery`)

Three strips (deep-sky, posters, insignia), each a link over a row of the collection's real thumbnails with a scrim and its text; the text starts on the page title's left edge. A strip's height is a minimum: the text sets it, so none of it is clipped. Posters and insignia keep the art beside the text (the text in the left 40%, the art from 40%); on a phone the art sits behind the text under a heavier scrim.

## 2 · The deep-sky viewer (`/gallery/deep-sky`)

The grid of tiles opens a full-window viewer. **Rule: the viewer fits one screen.** At 1366x768 and 1440x900 the image, the facts row (label above value), the credit line and the "show in the sky" button are inside the window with the image at least 300 px high, for every image and every locale. The image takes the height the caption stack leaves; on a phone, or in a window too short for both, the figure scrolls and every element stays reachable. The caption keeps italic at `--size-caption` (UXD-002); the facility line and designation are not teal. The button's text is the page background token on the accent gradient (4.96:1 on the dark end of the gradient).

## 3 · The note (`/gallery/deep-sky`, `/posters`, `/patches`, `/sourcing`)

Each page ends with one `<footer class="page-note">`: the shell's note, the same in all four (Design System §4.6). A page writes no footer rule of its own.

## Guards

`tests/e2e/gallery.spec.ts` (viewer fit at both laptop sizes, the phone scroll, the note's computed style across the four pages, the strips' text), `tests/e2e/page-shell.spec.ts` (the strip's left edge), `tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts` (the gallery rows).
