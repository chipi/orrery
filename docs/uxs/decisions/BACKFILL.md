# Backfill: UX decisions made before the UXD series

Extracted on 2026-11-11 from `IA.md`, the UXS documents, AGENTS.md, the design system doc, the ADRs
and the operator's operator notes. History, not re-decided: each row says what was decided and where it
is stated. A row becomes a full UXD only when the decision is revisited.

**How to read it.** **Kind** is one of `decided`, `superseded` and `rejected`. A `superseded` row
names the record or row that replaced it; "partly superseded" means a later record changed part of the
rule and the row stays `decided`. **Who / ref** is `operator` when the source says the operator made
the call (with the dated operator note), then any ADR, PRD or commit the source cites; it is `spec` when
the source gives neither. **Date** is copied from the source; `undated` means the source gives none
(the date is not invented). **Where stated** is file and line (or section) at commit `9fdf4cc4a6b`.
"Conflicts with" appears only where the code or another document was read and disagrees; the ids SC-1
to SC-5 are the spec-to-code mismatches in
[UXS-000](../UXS-000-orrery-ux-architecture.md#spec-to-code-mismatches-open). Operator notes are the
operator's private working notes (cited by name and date, with no path), quoted for the
decision and its date only.

## Notes on what this ledger does not contain

- **IA and UXS rules with no date and no stated decider.** The UXS documents are mostly "Draft
  v0.1" specifications; their individual rules (card layouts, panel anatomy, HUD values) carry no
  decision date. They are the current spec, not history, and are not enumerated here.
- **Decisions only in the working plan.** The 2026-11-11 answers (Q1, Q2 and the clarifications) are
  the UXDs, not backfill.
- **Open questions** (D1 to D7, Q3 to Q10) are listed in
  [UXS-000 §Open](../UXS-000-orrery-ux-architecture.md#open), not here.
- **ADRs that are architecture, not UX** (for example ADR-001 to ADR-017 on stack, ADR-063 to ADR-071
  on deploy) are not backfilled; the UX-relevant ones are rows above. The ADRs were found by title
  scan, not read end to end; only the ADRs cited in the rows were read for their decision text.
