- **Decider**: the operator, 2026-11-14 — the recommendations of `docs/wip/2026-11-13-slice-E4-brief.md` §12 approved as written (E-decisions 16a, 17a, 18a, 19a, 20a, 21a, "approved all E-series recommendations")
- **Answers**: FL2, FL3 of the UX feedback plan; slice E4
- **Builds on**: [UXD-014](UXD-014-one-chip-and-filter.md) (the chip and the FILTERS strip), [UXD-025](UXD-025-lists-carry-a-round-photo.md) (the round photo of a list row, T4), ADR-053 (amended: no portraits, no patches)

## Context

The line-up (a real 3D true-scale scene) was reachable only through the smallest control on /fleet: an 11 px label and two toggle chips under the FILTERS row, which set the category filter as a side effect and showed the way out ("Back to the grid") only afterwards. The panel's tab for craft with flights was called CREW, listed the entry's `flights`, and linked every row to `/missions?id=…` although 88 of 139 flights name a mission that does not exist; the OVERVIEW button counted `linked_missions` (Crew Dragon: "2 missions" against 4 rows), and an entry with one linked mission printed the raw mission id on that button (74 of 294 entries). Every crew card was the "—" circle: `portrait_path` is set on 0 of 42 crew rows, `patch_path` on 0 of 139 flights.

## Decision

1. **The line-up is a view (16a).** On the FILTERS row, after the FILTERS chip and never inside the collapsible strip, a `ChipGroup role="radiogroup"` named "View" holds GRID and LINE-UP (`fleet_view_grid`; the existing `fleet_lineup_toggle`, whose per-locale words already say "comparison at scale"). A second radiogroup (Launchers, Capsules & spacecraft) follows it only while LINE-UP is on. GRID is the way back, so the "Back to the grid" chip is gone. The list view (`?view=list`) is not part of the toggle.
2. **The line-up has its own kind and obeys no filter (17a).** The kind is its own state in the URL, `?view=lineup&kind=launchers|spacecraft`; choosing it no longer sets the category filter, so GRID returns with the filters as they were. Old links `?category=launcher&view=lineup` still open the launcher line-up (the kind is read from `category` when `kind` is absent). While LINE-UP is on, the FILTERS chip stays in place, disabled, and its panel and CLEAR FILTERS are not drawn: the line-up is a fixed true-scale comparison of one kind, and nothing on the row moves.
3. **One Missions tab for every entry (18a, 19a).** `fleetMissionRows(entry, missionIndex, missionName)` (pure, `src/lib/fleet-missions.ts`) returns one row per distinct mission in the union of `flights` and `linked_missions`, oldest first (the mission's year, else the first year in the flight designation). A row whose mission exists is a link (28 px round photo with the agency ring, the mission's name in the page locale, its year, an arrow to `/missions?id=<id>`); a row whose mission does not exist is plain text (the 8 px dot, the flight designation, its note), not a link, 70 % ink, no hover. The flight's crew is one line under the row, "CREW  Doug Hurley, Bob Behnken" (existing `panel_tab_crew`; roles dropped). A header line above the list reads "5 missions · 2 in the catalogue" (`fleet_missions_in_catalogue`), or "N missions" when every row is a link. The tab is called MISSIONS (`panel_tab_missions`) and shows from 2 rows; the "CREW" label, the portrait grid, the patch image and the empty portrait circle are deleted (ADR-053 amended).
4. **One count (20a).** The OVERVIEW button counts the same rows: 2 or more rows: "N missions →" opening the tab; exactly one row and its mission exists: the button links to it and prints the mission's name, not its id; one row with no mission: no button. `/fleet/<id>` lists the same rows under one "Missions" heading (links to `/missions/<id>`, the year, the mission's first, the crew line); the separate "Crew" section is gone.
5. **The six `gemini-N` ids (21a).** `gemini-3`, `-4`, `-6a`, `-7`, `-8`, `-12` in `gemini.json` became `gemini3`, `gemini4`, `gemini6a`, `gemini7`, `gemini8`, `gemini12`, the ids of the missions they point at. Gemini now lists 10 rows, 6 of them links. `fleet-missions.test.ts` fails if any flight names a mission that exists only after its hyphens are stripped. The other 88 dead ids stay: the missions do not exist (adding them is the content stream and the mission runbook).
6. **E3 is not built here.** The era strip and the agency palette of E3 are deferred to the end of the arc (the Higgsfield mock-ups). The view toggle sits on the FILTERS row, right of the FILTERS chip, so the strip can be added inside the collapsible filters panel, and the agency dots on the category chips later, without moving the toggle.

7. **The observatory card colour is not teal any more.** `CATEGORY_COLOR.observatory` on `/fleet` was `#4ecdc4` (teal); it is `#8fd16a`, the lowest-orbit band's green (`epoch-bands.ts`), as part of the operator's removal of the old teal outside the science diagrams ([UXD-001](UXD-001-ui-accent-blue-replaces-teal.md)). No test asserts the old value.

## Consequences

- Three new keys, `fleet_view_aria`, `fleet_view_grid`, `fleet_missions_in_catalogue`, in 14 locales, written by hand: "View" takes the word of the existing `fly_view_drawer` (the label audit fails on two translations of one English label), "Grid" each locale's existing word for the fleet card grid (`fleet_grid_aria`), "in the catalogue" the word each locale uses for the Mission Catalog. Two keys retired: `fleet_lineup_back`, `fleet_flight_mission_link`. The glossary has no entry for these words.
- Flight designations and crew names are English base-file text in every locale (not new, not changed).
- 11 of the 13 entries that have flights and no `linked_missions` (2 or more rows) now get the OVERVIEW button, though some of their rows are all plain text; a reader may prefer the button only where a row links (not decided).
- 2 entries (one flight, no mission in the catalogue, counted by script over the data) have 1 row and so no tab and no button; one of the two held crew names, which no longer show anywhere in the panel (`/fleet/<id>` lists the row).
- `fleet-missions.test.ts` guards the union count over every fleet entry, that only existing missions get an id, and the Gemini ids; `fleet-missions-view.spec.ts` guards the toggle, the old link, Crew Dragon (5 rows, 2 links, no placeholder), Gemini (10, 6), Soyuz 7K-OK (7, 3), that every link opens an existing mission, one-mission entries print a name, and `/fleet/crew-dragon`.
