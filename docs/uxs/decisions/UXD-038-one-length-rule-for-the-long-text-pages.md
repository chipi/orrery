## Context

**CR2 / CO2.** `/credits` drew every row of 42 sources (3185 photo rows, 54 text rows, 110 insignia, 69 video posters, 29 data entries; 3,393 images in the DOM) and carried a 43 MB image budget on every device. `/colophon` already showed 10 items per section and a "Show all" chip, which took it from 62.7 MB to 4.1 MB on a phone (2026-11-08).

## Decision

1. **One rule for every long list (24a).** A list draws its first `PREVIEW` = 10 rows (`previewList` in `$lib/preview-list`) and, while longer and not opened, a "Show all N" chip (`ShowAll`, an `action` `Chip`; text `colophon_show_all`). The source and section headers stay open. The opened state is runtime only (no storage). `/credits` applies it to every source's photo list and text list, the audio, video and insignia lists and the data catalogues; `/colophon` replaces its page-local `shown()` and snippet with the same two parts. The 69 video posters are `loading="lazy"` again: the cap removed the reason they were eager.
2. **Anchors and Ctrl-F.** A contents link (`#src-<id>`, `#sec-<id>`) lands on an open header with its first 10 rows below it, so no hash handler is needed. "Show all" sits at the foot of the list: the rows are inserted below the chip, so the first new row opens where the chip was (`credits.spec.ts` asserts the 11th row is in view after the click; scrollY itself moves by the browser's scroll anchoring when off-screen `content-visibility` blocks above settle to their real height, which is why it is not the thing asserted). Ctrl-F finds text in the rows that are drawn; a row beyond the tenth is found after "Show all", the cost of a cap that the closed-section alternative (24b) would have shared.
3. **The `/credits` contents list** (42 rows, 1,578 px high on a phone) is capped the same way below 768 px only (`ShowAll narrow` with `class="preview-narrow"` on the list, the rule in `ShowAll.svelte`); from 768 px it sits in two columns and stays whole. `/colophon`'s contents list (16 rows) is not capped. `/library` follows the rule in F2 (25a).
4. **Colophon totals line (27a).** `colophonSections()` in `$lib/colophon-sections` returns the sections the page draws, in page order, with their counts; the contents list, the heading counts and the line "N original works in K sections" (`colophon_totals`, `data-testid="colophon-totals"`, under the lead) read those rows, never the manifest's `totals` object, which leaves out the tour episodes and the icon grid. Interface graphics: the contents row said 4 (`data.ui`) and its heading 14 (the glyph registry); both now read the registry. The contents list now follows the page order (posters came before the mission trajectories in the page and after them in the list).
5. **Launcher sketches (26a).** Each `anatomy_art` entry has `kind: 'spacecraft' | 'launcher'`, set by `build-original-work.mjs`: `launcher` when the fleet entry's category is `launcher`, plus two drawings with no fleet entry (`m-v`, `pslv`) named in the script. 158 spacecraft cutaways and 40 launcher sketches (portrait 2:3 pencil, drawn as their own section with portrait cards, `colophon_section_launchers`); the exploded section keeps its 76. A `kind` field leaves every translated overlay (keyed by file) where it is. `colophon-sections.test.ts` fails on a new drawing that has no fleet entry until the script's list is looked at.
6. **Moon, Mars and Earth credits (28a).** `src/lib/credits-coverage.test.ts` fails when an image file under the Moon, Mars or Earth scene folders has no row in `image-provenance.json` (matched by stem as `/credits` bundles; a width rung is told from a name ending in digits by `image-ladder.json`). `scripts/audit-scene-image-credits.mjs` is the browser audit: it loads the three scenes and their first sites and compares every requested image to the manifest. A row is not a verified credit: the 71 placeholder credits stay on the repo's own ceiling (`validate-gallery-credits`).

## Verification

Unit: `preview-list.test.ts`, `colophon-sections.test.ts` (158 + 40 = 198 cutaways, launchers = the fleet's launchers plus `m-v` and `pslv`, the exploded 76 whole), `credits-coverage.test.ts` (9 folders, mutation-checked: adding `images/landing-sequences` makes it fail with 39 files), `validate-data`, `npm run check` (0 errors).

E2E on desktop-chromium, mobile-chromium and mobile-landscape-chromium against the dev server, `--workers=1`: `credits.spec.ts` (13) and the new `colophon.spec.ts` (4: the totals line equals the sum of the section counts on screen, the contents rows equal the headings, 158 / 40 / 76, Show all opens the rest, a contents link lands on its header with 10 rows below); on desktop-chromium also the `a11y`, `type-floor`, `chip`, `page-shell`, `accent-colour`, `smoke` and `video-media` rows of the two pages. Dev-server hydration timeouts and 500s in the first runs were rerun alone and passed.

Image weight after a full scroll (`image-weight.spec.ts`, dev server, not a production build): `/credits` 40.8 MB on a phone with every "Show all" opened (the old rendering, 3,393 images in the DOM) and 3.7 / 3.7 / 3.7 / 3.7 MB (phone / tablet / desktop / tv) as built (308 images), oversize 0; the budget moves from 43 to 5 on all four. `/colophon` is 5.0 / 5.0 / 2.9 / 4.8 MB (was 4.1 / 4.1 / 2.6 / 3.9 on 2026-11-08; not attributed between the launcher section and the art added since), so its budget moves from 5 / 5 / 3 / 5 to 6 / 6 / 4 / 6. The spec now waits for the page's own sources to render before it reads: before, a slow dev-server hydration read ~0 MB and passed.

CR3: `scripts/audit-scene-image-credits.mjs --sites=5` against the dev server (each scene and its first five sites, every panel tab opened): `/moon` 105 image requests, 72 stems; `/mars` 43, 30; `/earth` 51, 31; all credited on `/credits` or listed as Orrery's own art on `/colophon`; 0 with no credit, 0 external, 0 failed. A lower bound: a request the run did not trigger (a site beyond the fifth, a gallery image past the first tab) is not seen.

Not checked: Firefox and WebKit; Ctrl-F behaviour was reasoned, not tried; a production build; the other 13 locales' pages (the two keys and their numbers were not looked at on screen); whether each credit row is right, not only present.
