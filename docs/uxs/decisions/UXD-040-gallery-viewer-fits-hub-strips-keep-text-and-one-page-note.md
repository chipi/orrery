## Context

**G6.** On `/gallery/deep-sky` the lightbox figure was a flex column with `max-height: 90vh` and an image capped at `72vh`, so the caption stack below it (observatory line, title, designation, caption, facts row, credit line, "show in the sky" button, about 315 px) did not fit a laptop: at 1366x768 the image spanned y 38 to 591, the facts' labels sat at the bottom edge and every value (y 768 to 788), the credit line (y 860) and the button (y 886 to 920) were below the screen; at 1440x900 the credit line (y 962) and the button (y 988 to 1022) were. **G4.** The footer where sources are discussed existed as four footers in two designs (deep-sky and posters: a frame, 12 px Space Mono at 70% white; patches and sourcing: a top rule, 14 px at 60% white). **Hub strips (found while measuring, not an operator point).** On `/gallery` the posters and insignia strips clamped their text to two lines ("free to…", "with a sourcing wall for…") and the posters text, 596 px right edge, ran under the first thumbnail (left edge 512 px).

## Decision

1. **The viewer is a grid (42a; G6).** `.lb-figure` is `display: grid` with two rows, the image and the caption stack, `max-height: calc(100svh - 80px)`; the image takes the height the stack leaves (`max-height: 100%`, its own aspect kept), so the facts, the credit line and the button are always inside the window. The figure is `overflow: auto`: on a phone, and in a window too short for both (a phone in landscape), it scrolls instead of shrinking the picture away. The image row has a 200 px floor (`minmax(200px, 1fr)`, not `minmax(0, 1fr)`: with 0 the picture of a landscape phone, 358 px of figure height under a 330 px stack, would be about 30 px high). The caption stack's spacing is tightened by 4 px at five places (about 16 px in all). UXD-009's label above value stays; the facts were already `dt` over `dd`. The "show in the sky" button's text is `var(--color-bg)` (it was `#06231f`, a dark teal, 4.02:1 on the gradient's `#5070ff` end; `#04040c` is 4.96:1 on `#5070ff` and 8.32:1 on `#8aa0ff`). One page uses this viewer; the panel galleries have their own (`PanelLightbox`, not this slice).
2. **G2 to G4 mean `/gallery/deep-sky` (43a).** The page with the filter pills and the footer where sources are discussed. G2 and G3 were closed by UXD-012 and UXD-014 and are verified at HEAD, not redone; G4's colour was closed by UXD-001, its font is item 3.
3. **One `.page-note` (44a; G4).** `src/lib/styles/page-shell.css` holds one class for the footer of `/gallery/deep-sky`, `/posters`, `/patches` and `/sourcing`: a 1 px frame at 8% white with 4 px corners, `20px 24px` padding, 44 px above, Space Mono `--size-meta` (12 px), line-height 1.7, 70% white, `strong` and links in `--color-state` (the muted blue), links underlined. The four local footer rules are gone (`git grep -n "gallery-footer\|patches-footer\|sourcing-footer" src` returns nothing). `/patches` and `/sourcing` change from 14 px at 60% white under a rule to the framed 12 px note; `/gallery`'s `.aside` (a one-line pointer to the sourcing wall, not a footer of a collection) is not changed.
4. **The hub strips keep their text (found in F4).** On `/gallery` a strip's height is a minimum (`min-height`, was `height`) and the text is in flow, so it sets the band's height and the two-line clamp is gone; the posters and insignia strips (`.strip-beside`) hold their text in 40% of the width and start their art at 40%, so no text runs under a thumbnail (the art is clipped on the right); on a phone the art sits behind the text under a heavier scrim. The deep-sky strip keeps its photo behind the text and its scrim.

## Consequences

- A fifth gallery-family footer uses the class and writes no footer rule.
- The figure is a scroll container: the image's 60 px shadow is clipped at the figure's edge (it was invisible on the 92% dark backdrop).
- A strip with a longer translation grows taller; it is never cut.
