# UXD-042 — Plan to Fly: the chosen rocket and a payload carried across, a launch that starts, grouped destinations, one marker row on the play bar

- **Status**: accepted (built; checked by a new e2e spec on three projects, unit tests and screenshots, not yet by the operator)
- **Date**: 2026-11-14 (proposed and decided)
- **Decider**: the operator, 2026-11-14 — the recommendations of `docs/wip/2026-11-14-slice-G1-brief.md` §12 approved as written ("Yes on G1/2": decisions G-1a to G-9a)
- **Answers**: PL1, PL2, PL3, FY3 of the UX feedback plan; slice G1
- **Builds on**: [UXD-014](UXD-014-one-chip-and-one-filter-component.md) (the scrubber's play and speed chips), [UXD-001](UXD-001-ui-accent-blue-replaces-teal.md) (no teal: the beat ticks are the muted blue `#889fe7`)
- **Changes**: `src/lib/plan/rocket-handoff.ts`, `src/lib/plan/flight-destination.ts`, `src/lib/plan/destinations.ts`, `src/lib/fly/journey-markers.ts`, `src/lib/orbital/flight-plan-beats.ts` (all new except `destinations.ts`), `src/lib/fly-mission-apply.ts`, `src/lib/fly/apply-to-page.ts`, `src/lib/physics/ascent/launch-profile-registry.ts`, `src/lib/components/LaunchScene.svelte` (the flight-plan list reads the shared beats), `src/routes/plan/+page.svelte`, `src/routes/fly/+page.svelte`, 7 message keys in 14 locales, `tests/e2e/plan-to-fly.spec.ts` (new), `tests/e2e/chip.spec.ts`. No data file, no hero, no image.

## Context

A plan opened in Fly carried four things (`dest`, `type`, `dep`, `tof`) and never the rocket the user had picked; the flight's vehicle and payload read "—"; the opening card's START WITH LAUNCH button was drawn and did nothing (`resolveLauncher(undefined, '—')` returned `{ id: '' }`, so a launch looked available, and `loadLaunchProfile('')` returned null); nine of the 22 destinations (the planetary moons) fell through to the ORRERY DEMO scenario; the destination `select` was 22 flat rows ending on Bennu; and the play bar's two marker rows hid while a launch played and never showed the ascent or descent beats.

## Decision

1. **The rocket travels as `rocket=<rockets.json id>` (G-1a).** `/plan` writes it into its own URL once the user has picked one (a reload or a shared link keeps it; an incoming `?rocket=` is carried through until the list loads) and appends the rocket in effect to the FLY MISSION link, which is only enabled when that rocket's Δv budget holds. `/fly` reads it through one pure function, `resolveRocketHandoff` (`src/lib/plan/rocket-handoff.ts`): vehicle name (the name /plan shows) and a `fleet_refs` launcher ref. The three ids that differ from the launch registry's (`atlas-v-541`, `soyuz-fregat`, `proton-m`) are mapped explicitly and a unit test pins every rocket to the profile `resolveLauncher` picks: a flagship for twelve, the generic stand-in only for Ariane 6 (it has no profile JSON). An unknown id is ignored, as before.
2. **The default payload (G-2a).** "Generic spacecraft · N kg": the spacecraft the ascent already draws (`buildGenericPayload`), N the mass the ascent integrates (`profile.payloadKg` of the launcher's profile: Falcon Heavy 6065, SLS 26500, Atlas V 541 9800, 6000 for a generic profile). The opening card shows it; the HUD has no payload row.
3. **A launch that starts (G-3a).** The opening card's START WITH LAUNCH works for a plan flight and `?launch=1` works for a plan URL (the auto-start waits for the plan flight to be applied). The dead button is fixed at its cause: `resolveLauncher` returns null for a vehicle string that slugs to nothing, so a plan URL without `rocket=` offers no launch at all. A plan flight carries the destination enum (`dest`), so the injection burn is the right one (TLI, TMI, INJECTION).
4. **The nine moons fly (G-4a).** Every plan destination resolves (`resolvePlanFlightDestination`): the geocentric Moon keeps its cislunar flight; a planetary moon flies its host planet's arc (its porkchop rides the host's axes) with the moon named as the target ("EARTH → EUROPA via JUPITER · LANDING"). The data shape for the 3D scene is `LoadedMission.targetMoon` (a `MoonMissionDestId`, set only for a moon flight), next to `activeDestination` (the host): the scene draws the moon from it (slice G2).
5. **No descent markers for plan flights (G-5a).** Descent profiles are keyed by mission id; a plan LANDING flight ends at arrival. Descent beats appear for the missions that have a profile.
6. **Five destination groups (G-6a, G-7a).** Inner system (Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Phobos, Deimos), Asteroids (Vesta, Ceres, Psyche, Bennu), Jupiter system (Jupiter, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto), Saturn system (Saturn, Titan, Enceladus), Outer system (Uranus, Neptune, Triton, Pluto). The control stays the native `select` with one `<optgroup>` per group (the phone's own picker, free accessibility, the pattern `/lab` already uses). `PLAN_DESTINATION_GROUPS` is the one source; `PLAN_DESTINATION_IDS` (the body panels' "Plan mission" chip) is derived from it, in group order.
7. **One marker row for the journey (G-8a, G-9a).** The two cruise rows become one: ascent beats (liftoff, MAX-Q, stage sep, fairing, SECO, and the parking orbit, injection burn and spacecraft separation when the mission leaves orbit), cruise milestones (gold dots) and flight-director stages (gold diamonds), descent beats where a profile exists. Ascent and descent beats are 2 px ticks in `#889fe7` with a label on hover and focus; a click seeks there (through the same scrub the bar uses, so a tick lands where the same spot on the bar would); the row shows during the launch (the old `!showLaunch` condition is gone). Ticks closer than 10 px on the measured track collapse into the first, which carries a `+N` in its label: the ascent slice is about 166 px wide at 1440 px and 32 px on a 390 px touch phone, where six beats cannot all show. Positions come from the master clock (`pointToScrubber`), so the cruise band ends where the descent tail begins. The beats are one source, `src/lib/orbital/flight-plan-beats.ts`, shared with the launch HUD's FLIGHT PLAN list; labels go through `hudLabel` (flight-dynamics acronyms stay as they are in every locale); the accessible name is the one new template `fly_beat_aria` ("{label} at {time}. Click to jump."), written by hand in 14 locales. The cruise milestones keep `fly_milestone_aria` and the row keeps the `milestone-track` test id.

## Consequences

- The ADR-026 §FLY-button experience URL is now `/fly?dest&type&dep&tof&rocket` (no mission id; `rocket` is optional).
- The group labels are 5 new keys, `fly_payload_generic` and `fly_beat_aria` the other two, 7 × 14 strings.
- A longer group label in `de`, `ru` or `hi` widens the select's open list, not the closed control (the closed select shows the destination).
- A moon flight has the host planet's arc, not a multi-leg flight (host flyby then moon capture): that is a larger arc, not part of G1.

## Verification

Unit: the rocket mapping (13 ids, three differing, unknown), destination resolution (22 ids, nine moons to their hosts, Europa on Jupiter), the registry guard (`resolveLauncher(undefined, '—') === null`, red before the fix), the payload line, the plan apply with and without a handoff, the groups (5, 22, each once), the marker positions with and without a descent tail, the declutter at 216 and 1109 px. E2E: `tests/e2e/plan-to-fly.spec.ts` (desktop-chromium, mobile-chromium, mobile-landscape-chromium): a Plan click with Falcon Heavy shows the rocket and "Generic spacecraft · 6065 kg"; START WITH LAUNCH mounts the launch scene; ascent markers exist and seek; `?launch=1` works for Jupiter and the Moon; no rocket means no launch button; Europa, Phobos, Titan and Triton name the moon and not the demo.

Not checked: a launch from a plan flight to the end (the warp into the cruise at the plan's departure day, the Moon's cislunar hand-in) was seen to start, not watched through; Ariane 6 flies the generic profile; the 14-locale widths of the group labels were not measured in a browser; no descent marker was exercised on a plan flight (there is none), the descent row is covered by the unit test only.
