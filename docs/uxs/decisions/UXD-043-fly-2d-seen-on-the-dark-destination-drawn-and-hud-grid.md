## Context

**FY1.** `draw2d` painted on `#04040c` with 1 px rings at 0.35 alpha, stars at 0.06 to 0.22 and not-yet-flown arcs at 0.2. Composited over the background they measured: Earth ring 1.71:1, Mars ring 1.38:1, stars 1.09 to 1.62:1, future arcs 1.21:1 and 1.23:1. The view also drew Earth and Mars for every flight, at a fixed `min(W, H) / 4` px per AU: 15 of 21 heliocentric destinations were off the canvas at 1440x900 (Jupiter 1,171 px from the Sun against a 450 px half-height), and the arrival ring of a Jupiter flight was Mars-coloured. **FY4.** `.hud-row` was `display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap`, so a value one character too long dropped whole under its label ("TRAJECTORY" alone on a line, "Heliocentric Hohmann" under it). In 3D the nine moon destinations had textures in `static/textures/` that `/fly` never loaded.

## Decision

1. **Neutral rings, brighter lines (10a).** One const object, `FLY_2D_STYLE` in `src/lib/fly/fly-2d-style.ts`, holds every 2D line: rings (Earth, the destination, the Moon's) `rgba(255,255,255,0.45)` at 1.25 px; the LAUNCH / ARRIVAL anchor rings white at 0.7; stars alpha 0.15 to 0.51 (`0.15 + (i % 5) * 0.09`); the labels 11 px at 85 % white (were 9 px). The planet identity colour left the rings and stays on the live body, its halo and its label. **Departure from the approved numbers:** the approved future-arc alpha of 0.45 measures 1.82:1 (outbound `#5070ff`) and 1.91:1 (return `#9966ff`), under the same decision's 3:1 floor, so the not-yet-flown arcs are 0.75 alpha (3.19:1 outbound, 3.51:1 return). The flown arcs and the dash pattern are unchanged.
2. **The scale fits the flight (11a).** `fitScale2d(extentAu, W, H)` puts the outermost of {the destination's orbit, the farthest trajectory point} at 85 % of the shorter half-extent; the extent never goes below 1.5 AU (a Venus trip does not zoom in on the Sun). The scale eases toward the fit with a 0.27 s time constant (95 % in about 0.8 s; reduced motion snaps). A readout at the bottom left draws a 1 AU bar and "1 AU = N px" (`fly_2d_scale_label`, parameter `px`, hand-written in 14 locales with each locale's unit symbol from the i18n style guide: au, AE, а. е., АЈ, و.ف). The phase markers use the same fitted scale. The Moon (cislunar) view keeps its own scale.
3. **Only the active destination (12a).** `select2dTargets` chooses what is drawn besides Earth, the origin: the active destination (ring from `orbitPath`, live body from `destinationPos`, disc radius from `DEST_STYLE` clamped to 3.5 to 9 px, halo and label in `DESTINATION_LABEL_COLORS[id]`, label `destinationLabel(id)`); for a planetary-moon flight (`mission.targetMoon`) the host planet plus the named moon on a short ring; for a Sun-target mission no destination body. The hard-coded Mars ring, Mars disc and `nav_mars` label are gone; the arrival ring is white and sits where the trajectory ends.
4. **3D moons (13a).** The nine moon destinations (Phobos, Deimos, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Enceladus, Triton) load their existing `2k_<id>.jpg` when first shown or targeted (not at build, so a flight that never shows moons pays nothing). `setTargetMoon` gives the targeted moon an orbit ring in `#889fe7` at 0.9 opacity, a 1.6x body, and keeps it visible when the moon layer is off. Ceres, Vesta, Psyche and Bennu stay stylised spheres; no asset was added.
5. **A label/value grid (14a).** Every label/value block in `/fly` is a two-column grid, `fit-content(50%) minmax(0, 1fr)` with a 10 px gap: the `.hud` panels (identity, navigation, flight parameters, systems; their non-row children span both columns), the spacecraft info card (`dl`), and the CAPCOM communications rows (`.comm-grid`). The row element is `display: contents`, so the label column is shared by every row of a panel, the value is left-aligned, starts in the label's row and wraps only inside its own column (a long single token still breaks anywhere). The search `flex-wrap` on `.hud-row` returns nothing; the other `flex-wrap` rules in `/fly` are engine dots, status lights and chip rows, not label/value rows. The mini HUD's `.mh-row` is three unlabelled values and the telemetry console's headings are titles, so they stay flex.

## Consequences

- A 2D flight to Pluto shows the Earth ring as a 10 px circle: the compression is the true picture, and the readout says so.
- A moon flight's 2D moon dot circles its host at the moon's real period, so a Phobos flight spins fast at high sim speeds (the 3D moons use a wall-clock drift for this reason; 2D has no such decoupling).
- The 3D moon highlight has no text label (the HUD title names the moon).
- `.hud` panels are grids, so `.hud-phase` uses `justify-self`, and a new non-row child of a `.hud` panel spans both columns by the `.hud > :not(.hud-row)` rule.
- The stars at the low end (0.15 alpha, 1.33:1) are dots for depth, not graphics the viewer needs; only the strongest stars clear 3:1.
