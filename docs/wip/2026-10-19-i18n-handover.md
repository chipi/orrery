## The decision: what "done" means

The operator asked what the end of this exercise is. Agreed definition:

- Only **BROKEN** counts (wrong meaning, missing text, garbled, English left in). MINOR is style
  and is accepted.
- **Done** = no locale × segment cell with **≥ 20% BROKEN** in the 2026-10-19 sample.
- After that: **no more sampling rounds.** Quality is held where errors enter: the translate-time
  checks, fixing specific strings when someone spots them, and Hebrew (phase 3) built with the
  glossary and rules from the start.
