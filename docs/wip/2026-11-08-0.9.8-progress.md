## Questions for the operator

(added as they come up; nothing here blocks the run)

1. **Hub links for crawlers.** /catalog, /learn, /worlds, /explore/hub have no link in any
   prerendered page (only the closed nav dropdowns, TV mode, and the sitemap). Adding the four
   as plain links in the footer is a visible change (and a Linux visual-baseline regen). Yes?
2. **Edge cache for HTML.** Cloudflare does not cache HTML without a Cache Rule; an origin
   header alone changes nothing at the edge. With a rule (e.g. 5 min), every prod deploy must
   purge it, or cached pages point at deleted `/_app/immutable` chunks. Recommendation: leave
   HTML uncached until a purge step is in `deploy-prod.yml`; then add the rule. Want that?
3. **SEO titles beside the poetic H1s** (13 essays, 42 programs, ~45 science articles): the
   proposals are in `docs/wip/2026-11-08-seo-review/content.md`; they need your per-page yes.
4. **Panels → entity pages.** The `?id=` panels and the `/missions` / `/fleet` grids do not link
   to the new pages yet, and program rosters still link to `/missions?id=`. Options: a small
   "page" link in each panel header (visible change, 14 locales, one new label), and/or
   prerendering a plain list of entity links on `/missions` and `/fleet`. Which, if any?
5. **ADR-096** is written as Proposed; accept it?
6. **Thin entity pages:** some small-body and Venus-site pages are 50–130 words because that is
   all the translated data there is. Keep them indexable, or noindex pages under a word floor?
