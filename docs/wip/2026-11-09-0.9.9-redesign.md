# 0.9.9 — redesign: the site-wide teal accent

Operator decision 2026-11-09: 0.9.9 is about exactly this. Nothing here ships in 0.9.8.

## Why

The operator ruled out cyan/teal for UI states ("out of palette", "so fake"). 0.9.8 replaced the
surface-scene states with the muted blue `#889fe7` (derived from `--color-tier-core` `#7b9cff`):
marker hover (`HOVER_BLUE`), the ACTIVE status tone, the start of the route sol ramp and the 3D
outline pass (commit `3757791a334`). The same teal, `#4ecdc4` / `rgba(78,205,196,…)`, is also the
site's brand accent: about 780 uses in 147 files under `src/` (count from the 0.9.8 sweep).

## Scope to decide in 0.9.9

- The accent itself: `--color-teal` and `--color-tier-intro` in `src/lib/styles/tokens.css`, and the
  hard-coded `#4ecdc4` uses (chrome, nav, tabs, headings, chips, `/explore` HUD chips, the
  `SurfaceFlatPatch` detail-footprint box and banner text, home-page icons, footer links).
- Which teal uses are science data, not UI, and stay: the Moon tidal-lock overlay
  (`routes/moon/+page.svelte`), the Earth limb glow and tides (`earth-launch-sites-config.ts`),
  microgravity axes, Lab canvases (`TEAL`), fly-trajectory velocity arrows
  (`explore-solar-scene.ts`, `fly-*`), universe scenes, `epoch-bands`. The /science diagrams and the
  Lab goal art keep their style (operator, 2026-11-09).
- Status tones: ACTIVE `#889fe7` and PLANNED `#7b9cff` are near-identical; 0.9.8 separates them by
  a dashed PLANNED outline. Revisit with the new palette.
- Hover and the annotation-ring accent now share `#889fe7`.

- Colour pipeline: DONE (`8e9a7c8d1d9`). The surface EffectComposer had no OutputPass, so material
  colours reached the screen as linear values (#889fe7 showed as rgb 63,88,204). It now ends in an
  OutputPass (`outputPass: true` in `createOutlinePassSetup`, set by `SurfaceScene`), the
  `hoverBlue(composerActive)` workaround from 0.9.8 is gone, and high and low tiers show the same
  bytes (#889fe7 reads 136,156,228). Photos and the sol ramp are lighter on the composer tiers, as
  they already were on low/minimal. Open: `/iss` and `/tiangong` share the helper without the pass
  (their renderer sets ACES tone mapping it would newly apply) and are still unencoded.
