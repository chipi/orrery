## 4. Proposal
### 4.1 The viewer fits (G6; Decision 42)
`.lb-figure`: `display: grid; grid-template-rows: minmax(0, 1fr) auto; max-height: calc(100svh - 80px)` (`- 32px` at 480 px and below); `img`: `min-height: 0; max-height: 100%; object-fit: contain`; `figcaption` keeps its rules, its margin and the facts spacing tightened by about 10 px; `overflow-y: auto` on the figure for phones and very short windows. The image height is whatever the caption leaves, so a long caption or a long locale cannot hide the facts again. The "show in the sky" button text colour moves to a token (§0.6).
### 4.2 One note style under the gallery pages (G4 font; Decision 44)
A shared class `.page-note` in `src/lib/styles/page-shell.css` (the shell file, UXD-012): top rule or frame (one of them), Space Mono 12 px at 70% white (the framed variant's values, because it is already the gallery's reference), `strong` and links in `--color-state`; the four footers drop their local rules. If Decision 44b, only the deep-sky and posters copies are merged and patches and sourcing stay.
### 4.3 What does not change
The strips, the masonry, the chips, the lead, the caption's italic, the lightbox's controls, the thumbnails, any data. G1 to G3 are verified at HEAD, not re-done.
