a3-keep palette review

Every colour in the code tagged a3-keep after the teal accent was retired (UXD-001), grouped by the reason in its tag. Plan row D3 asks, per group: move to the new palette, or keep. The standing rules: no cyan or teal in UI states, rings, dioramas or reference maps; science diagrams keep their style. Teal and cyan swatches carry an outline. Each group shows a recommendation; the decision is yours.
