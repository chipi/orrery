(HTTP 403) were not readable, so there is no first-party NG-2 T+ table.

## Mission

| Figure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | 13 Nov 2025, 3:55 pm EST (20:55 UTC), LC-36 Cape Canaveral SFS, New Glenn NG-2 | PK, NL |
| Booster | GS1 "Never Tell Me the Odds" | SPO |
| Spacecraft | twins Blue and Gold, Rocket Lab; dry 209 kg, wet 535 kg (each, per the design block) | PK |
| Cost | under $80 million | SC |
| Lead | PI Robert Lillis, UC Berkeley SSL; NASA SIMPLEx (2019), Heliophysics Division | PK, NASA deployment blog |
| Trajectory | ~1 year loiter around Sun-Earth L2 ("kidney bean"); powered Earth flybys 7 Nov (Blue) and 9 Nov 2026 (Gold) | PK, NF |
| Mars | orbit insertion burns 7 and 9 Sep 2027, ~11 min at 450 km into a 60 h capture orbit; science from June 2028 | NF, PK |
