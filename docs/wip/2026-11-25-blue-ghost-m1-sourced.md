## Descent and landing
| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Site | Mare Crisium, near Mons Latreille | NASA release above |
| Coordinates | LROC: 18.5623 N, 61.8103 E, elevation -3650 m | LROC caption https://www.lroc.im-ldi.com/images/1407 (opened). Wikipedia pre-landing 18.56 N, 61.81 E. |
| Landing accuracy | within the 100 m landing target | Firefly https://fireflyspace.com/news/firefly-aerospace-becomes-first-commercial-company-to-successfully-land-on-the-moon/ |
| DOI | ~63 min before touchdown, 100 km altitude (~07:30 UTC, conv.) | Space.com https://www.space.com/space-exploration/private-spaceflight/firefly-blue-ghost-moon-landing-what-time-livestream ; collectSPACE https://www.collectspace.com/news/news-030225a-firefly-blue-ghost-mission-one-moon-landing.html |
| Coast | ~50 min, down to ~20 km (12.5 mi) altitude | collectSPACE above |
| Powered descent initiation (PDI) | 2025-03-02 2:22 a.m. CST = 08:22 UTC (conv.); lasts ~9 min; "D'Souza guidance" (Apollo-like); all engines; velocity cut from ~1,700 m/s to ~40 m/s | Firefly live updates above |
| Same event, other units | braking burn: ~5,500 ft/s to 130 ft/s (about 1,676 to 40 m/s); RCS to about 3 ft/s before touchdown | search-result snippet of Firefly/CBS reporting; page not opened. Space.com: D'Souza guidance 11 min before touchdown at 500 m (1,640 ft), 100 s before: terminal guidance at 10 m (33 ft) |
| Main engine shutoff | 2:32 a.m. CST = 08:32 UTC (conv.), under 2 min before touchdown; collectSPACE: shut off with 100 s remaining. Altitude at cutoff: conflicting (a search snippet says 500 ft / 1,600 m; Space.com lists 500 m for the D'Souza phase start and 10 m for 100 s before touchdown; these cannot all be right). Not resolved. | Firefly live updates; collectSPACE |
| Terminal descent | RCS thrusters pulsing to ~1 m/s descent rate; vision system tracks craters and slopes; "about 32 ft (10 m) at an expected 2 mph (1 m/s)" | collectSPACE; Firefly live updates |
| Touchdown | 2025-03-02 3:34 a.m. EST = 08:34 UTC | NASA release; SCALPSS paper ("approximately 08:34 UTC"); NSSDCA says 08:45 UT: conflict, 08:34 has 3 sources |
| Touchdown speed (actual) | not found. Planned: ~1 m/s | |
| Final attitude | "upright, stable configuration" (inertial readings) | Firefly landing release above |
| Hover or hazard-avoidance hold | Terrain-relative navigation and hazard avoidance in the last hour with 2 vision cameras; no numbered hover phase in any source | NASA press kit; Firefly mission page |
| Landing-site footage | NASA SCALPSS 1.1 four short-focal-length cameras, 8 fps. Footage begins ~91 ft (28 m); plume-surface interaction onset ~49 ft (15 m). The SFL cameras recorded from 30 m AGL to 10 s after touchdown; long-focal-length cameras 10 fps from 50 m to 5.5 m; 1,818 descent images | NASA https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasa-cameras-on-blue-ghost-capture-first-of-its-kind-moon-landing-footage/ ; NTRS SCALPSS paper above |
