# Cinematic look references (operator, 2026-11-25)

The operator sent phone screenshots of film stills and mission photos as the target look for /fly.
More batches are coming; each one is added here. The images themselves stay out of the repo (film
stills are not ours to ship); the session upload folder holds them.

## Batch 1: what the stills show

| Source | Shot | What to take from it |
|---|---|---|
| Apollo 13 (1995) | Saturn V on the pad beside the red umbilical tower, looking up; straight-down shot of liftoff with the steam cloud spreading over the pad roads | Low angle up the vehicle with the tower in frame; an overhead liftoff shot where the exhaust cloud spreads over the ground |
| For All Mankind | LM descending in a dust storm with light shafts; LRV on the Moon with Earth up; shuttle on a carrier aircraft over the clouds; Pathfinder shuttle | Lit dust and light shafts in a powered landing; the camera low on the surface; clouds below the craft for scale |
| Ad Astra | Rocket over the Florida coast from high altitude, boosters peeling away; launch tower in dust and smoke; ship lit by a low sun over Mars | Separations seen from altitude with the coastline below; a hard low sun with strong contrast |
| Gravity | ISS and Soyuz over Earth; astronaut close to camera with the station behind | Earth filling the background; the subject large in the foreground, depth from the station behind |
| The Martian | Ares MAV on Mars in orange haze; Hermes over Earth | Atmospheric haze tinted by the planet; the craft read as a silhouette against the sky |
| The Expanse | Rocinante in black space, a flip and burn; bright blue drive plumes | A dark ship lit by its own engine glow; the burn as the light source |
| 2001, Interstellar | Station wheel with the shuttle small beside it; Endurance ring on black | Scale from a small craft beside a large one; clean black space, hard sunlight |
| Alien | Nostromo landing lights through fog, from below | A big craft seen from underneath, its landing lights carving fog |
| Real: SpaceX | Falcon 9 landing legs and plume at sunset; Super Heavy caught by the tower chopsticks | A tracking camera on the landing stage; the plume lit against a low sky |
| Real: Blue Ghost M1 | The lander's shadow across the regolith at sunset, Earth small above | The shadow shot: the craft known only by its shadow, a tiny Earth in the sky |
| Real: Odysseus (IM-1) | Descent camera looking down the legs at the surface; the plume on the regolith | An onboard camera on a leg looking down at the final metres |
| Real: Curiosity, Perseverance | Selfies on Mars; Perseverance hanging under the sky crane, seen from the descent stage | The sky-crane shot from above, cables to the rover, dust below |
| Real: Apollo 11, 15, 17 | AS11-40-5927 (the LM on the surface), Irwin's salute with the LRV, the LM rising seen from the CSM | Surface stills as composition guides for the landed and way-home shots |

## How it is used

Each look becomes a candidate shot in the descent and launch directors (F7 framing, F8 clock),
one per row above, proposed to the operator before it is built. Nothing here is scheduled yet.
