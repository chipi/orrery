# Earth entry references: flown values, sourced (2026-11-25)

Research for the /fly US Standard Atmosphere 1976 retune. Every figure below was read in a document opened or downloaded in this session; anything else is "not found". "Derived" means I subtracted two quoted times; the source does not state the interval. No value was filled from memory.

Conventions:
- Mercury and Gemini sources time the start of entry at the 0.05 g relay (about 80 km), not at 400,000 ft. Where I write "EI" for those flights it is the 0.05 g point unless a 400,000 ft time is quoted.
- GET = ground elapsed time from liftoff. MSK = Moscow time (UTC+3).
- "Speed / FPA" is blank ("nf") for almost every flight: published postflight reports carry insertion and burnout speeds, not entry-interface state, except Apollo.

## Notes on conflicts and gaps

1. **Gemini 3 miss**: 60 nmi (GS, NASA conference paper) vs 45 nmi / 84 km (Wikipedia, "37 nmi off target" in-flight). Use 60 nmi with the L/D loss; Wikipedia is last-tier.
2. **Gemini 4 miss**: GS 44 nmi from the intended point; G4 final trajectory lands ~46 nmi from the plan by coordinates (derived by me); G4 also says the final landing is 50 nmi short of the preliminary prediction (a different baseline). Wikipedia 80 km (43 nmi). All within 3 nmi; use 44 nmi.
3. **Gemini 4 400,000 ft time**: the crew event table says 97:42:31, the text says full lift held until 97:42:52.5 at "approximately 400,000 ft". 21 s inside one report.
4. **Gemini 4 timeline vs planned**: the planned-vs-actual table shows landing 97:56:12 against 97:56:50 planned; peak g 7.7 final vs 6.8 planned.
5. **Gemini 6A miss**: GS 7 nmi vs Wikipedia 18 km (11 mi, about 10 nmi). Use 7 nmi.
6. **Gemini 7 peak g**: OCR renders the tracking value as "L.5" and the telemetry value as "U.5", both read as 4.5; confirm against the PDF image before using. Planned value is unreadable.
7. **Gemini 12 miss**: 2.6 nmi from the planned landing point (summary text) vs 3.8 nmi from the target point (recovery section) in the same report; the target and planned points differ slightly. Use 2.6 for "miss from plan", 3.8 for "from target".
8. **Mercury 0.05 g is not 400,000 ft**: do not treat it as EI in the model without converting. M3 and M2 do not give the altitude of the relay in readable text; sources list the 0.05 g relay as the "begin reentry" event. Mercury EI speed and FPA are not in any document I could read (the speed/FPA tables in M1 and M2 came out of OCR as blanks).
9. **MA-6 timing**: M2 gives only minute resolution (0.05 g T+4:43, peak T+4:47, main T+4:50, landing T+4:55). The OCR of the event table is empty; the numeric table is not recoverable from the text layer.
10. **Voskhod 2 off-target distance**: R2 says "more than 800 km overflight"; search-result snippets of Wikipedia/other pages say the capsule landed 386 km from the intended zone. These are different measures (overflight of the planned area vs distance between planned and actual point is one reading) and I did not open a page that reconciles them. Treat both as unreconciled.
11. **Soyuz 1 planned landing**: R3 as returned by the fetch says "150 km east of Karaganda"; the same fetch says recovery shifted to Orenburg and landing was projected 50 km E of Orsk. The planned point and the actual site (3 km W of Karabutak, snippet) are not reconciled by an opened source.
12. **Soyuz 11 retrofire time**: SF "01:35:24" and WK "22:35 GMT" agree if the SF time is Moscow time (UTC+3); I did not open a page that states the zone.
13. **Vostok 1 peak g**: R1 quotes Gagarin's greying, no number; 8-9 g and 8 g are Wikipedia only. For Vostok 2-6, Voskhod 1-2 and Soyuz 1/11 no peak g was found anywhere.
14. **Apollo FPA column**: the A1 table labels FPA "(deg E of N)", which reads like a heading unit; the values -2.07 and -1.74 are in the range of entry flight-path angles, but confirm against the Apollo 7 and 9 mission reports (A07 report PDF was not retrievable in this session).
15. **Skylab numbering**: SK is the "second visit" report (Skylab 3, SL-3). NTRS 19740027126 is the third visit (Skylab 4); the SL-2 first-visit report was not found by search.

### Biggest gaps (what is not found)

- **Entry-interface speed and flight-path angle** for every flight except Apollo 7 and 9 (and none of the Mercury, Gemini, Vostok, Voskhod, Soyuz, Shenzhou, Dragon, Starship). Gemini mission reports list insertion/burnout and reentry deceleration but not the 400,000 ft state; the reconstructed trajectory tables are in the reports' trajectory appendices, which I did not reach.
- **Peak g** for MA-7, MA-9, Gemini 3, 6A, 8, Vostok 1-6, Voskhod 1-2, Soyuz 1/11, Shenzhou 1, Skylab 2-4, ASTP, Dragon, Starship.
- **Drogue/main/landing intervals** for all Soviet/Russian/Chinese flights, Gemini 3/6A/8, MA-7, MA-9, Skylab, ASTP, Dragon.
- **Gemini mission reports not obtained**: Gemini III, VI-A, VIII, only referenced via the summary conference and Wikipedia (the NASA alsj PDF URL for Gemini VIII returned an empty body; ibiblio has no direct file for 3, 6A, 8).
- **Apollo 7/9 L/D and EI time-to-landing** are derived by me; no source states a drogue time.
- **Dragon (Inspiration4, Polaris Dawn) and Starship**: only splashdown/burn times from Wikipedia; SpaceX or NASA publication of peak g or heating was not found.
- **Skylab 2/4, ASTP**: landing times from Wikipedia only.
