## Launch
- 6 Feb 2018, 20:45 UTC, LC-39A, Kennedy Space Center - [WFT], [HOR]. Planned window 1:30-4:00 pm EST (18:30-21:00 UTC); the 20:45 UTC liftoff is therefore inside the 21:00 close - [KIT] (window); exact liftoff second: NOT FOUND.
- Side cores flight-proven: one flew Thaicom 8 (May 2016), the other CRS-9 (July 2016) - [KIT]. Serials: side B1023.2 (ex Thaicom 8, 27 May 2016) and B1025.2 (ex CRS-9, 18 Jul 2016), centre B1033.1 new, second stage new - [WFT] only (fetch summary).
- Block 3 / Block 4 labels for these cores: NOT FOUND (the only "Block" hits in [WFT] concern Falcon 9 versions).
- Thrust: 27 Merlin engines, "more than 5 million pounds of thrust" - [KIT], [NASA]; [WFT] "five million pounds-force (22 MN)". Liftoff mass: NOT FOUND.
- Planned mission: land all three cores; second stage to place the Roadster in a "precessing Earth-Mars elliptical orbit around the sun" - [KIT].
## Timeline (MET, hh:mm:ss) - [KIT] and [SFN] agree line for line; both are the pre-launch plan ("all times approximate"). [WFT]'s table repeats the same values. Separately recorded actual times: NOT FOUND.
- 00:01:06 max-Q. 00:02:29 BECO (side cores). 00:02:33 side cores separate. 00:02:50 side-core boostback burn starts (three engines each, per [SFN]). 00:03:04 centre-core MECO. 00:03:07 centre core/second stage separation. 00:03:15 second-stage start. 00:03:24 centre-core boostback. 00:03:49 fairing deployment.
- 00:06:41 side-core entry burn. 00:06:47 centre-core entry burn. 00:07:58 side-core landings (one time for both, LZ-1 and LZ-2, Cape Canaveral). 00:08:19 centre-core landing (target: drone ship Of Course I Still Love You). 00:08:31 SECO-1. 00:28:22 restart. 00:28:52 SECO-2. Then "an experimental long coast and third stage two burn" - [KIT].
- Separate landing times for LZ-1 vs LZ-2: NOT FOUND (single 00:07:58 entry). Landing-burn start times: NOT FOUND. "Nearly simultaneous" parallel touchdowns at LZ-1/LZ-2: [NASA] ("spectacular parallel touchdowns").
- Centre core: two of three landing engines failed to ignite; hit the water at 500 km/h (300 mph), 100 m from the ship; out of TEA-TEB igniter fluid; two of the drone ship's station-keeping thrusters damaged - [WFT]. [SPC2]: 300 mph (484 km/h), missed by about 328 ft (100 m); Musk tweet "Not enough ignition fluid to light the outer two engines after several three engine relights."; thruster damage on the ship. ## Payload
- Elon Musk's "midnight-cherry Tesla Roadster" as mass simulator; steel/concrete blocks normally flown - [KIT].
- Model year conflict: "2008" [WFT]; "2010 Tesla Roadster", "Musk personally owned the 2010 Roadster" [WTR]. Unresolved. [KIT] gives no year.
- Starman: mannequin in a SpaceX suit at the wheel, hand on the wheel and elbow on the sill - [WFT], [NASA]. Three cameras on the car/adapter; feed lasted just over 4 h ("batteries died four hours after launch" [NASA]); Musk estimated over 12 h - [WTR].
- Dashboard: Hot Wheels Roadster with a mini Starman; "DON'T PANIC" sign (both [WTR]); Hitchhiker's Guide in the glove box; towel reference [WFT]. Arch Mission Foundation 5D optical disc with Asimov's Foundation trilogy ([WTR], disc described as donated by Arch); circuit board "Made on Earth by humans"; employee plaque - [WTR]/[WFT].
- Mount: "permanently mounted on the rocket in an inclined position above the payload adapter" [WTR]; "custom-made payload adapter ... conventional fairing" [WFT]. Adapter design/mass: NOT FOUND.
- Mass conflict: launch mass about 1,300 kg, 5,900 kg with upper stage - [WTR]; "approximately 1,250 kg (with batteries)", about 800 kg without - Horizons object-data fetch summary (not re-queried). [WFT]: not given.
