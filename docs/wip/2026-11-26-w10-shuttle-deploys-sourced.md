## Common hardware (all six)
- Payload bay: 4.6 x 18 m - [ESA]. 15 x 60 ft (18.3 m) - search summary only. 18.3 m itself: NOT FOUND in an opened page.
- RMS (Canadarm): 15.2 m (50 ft) long, 38 cm (15 in) diameter, six joints: shoulder yaw and pitch, elbow pitch, wrist pitch, yaw, roll; booms graphite composite; end effector at the wrist grapples a payload fixture with a three-snare-wire design - [CARM]. Mass: 410 kg arm alone / 450 kg system [CARM]; [PK41] lists "Remote Manipulator System (payload bay) 1,180" lb (about 535 kg): the two disagree (the kit figure probably includes bay hardware; not verified).
- IUS: two-stage solid, 17 ft long, 9.25 ft diameter, about 32,500 lb - [PK34] (IUS-19), [PK30], [PK93] (IUS-27). Stage 1 about 42,000 lbf, stage 2 about 18,000 lbf - [PK34]. For STS-41 the IUS size is NOT FOUND in [PK41]/[MR41] (assume same family; unverified).
- Tilt table / ASE: tilt to 29 deg first to extend payload just outside the bay for checkout, then to the deployment angle, then released by ordnance, springs eject at about 6 in/s (about 0.15 m/s), table then lowered to minus 6 deg; orbiter RCS inhibited during release - [PK34], [PK30].
- Deployment angle: [PK34] 58 deg (Galileo); [PK93] 58 deg (Chandra); [MCD] steps 4 deg, 29 deg, 58 deg (Chandra); [PK30] 52 deg (Magellan). CONFLICT 52 vs 58 side by side; whether Magellan's ASE differed or the kit text was updated: NOT FOUND. STS-41 angle: NOT FOUND.
- IUS sequence (kits): about 10 min after release the IUS computer starts mission events and enables its RCS; about 45 min after release the first-stage ordnance inhibits come off, orbiter belly oriented to the IUS to shield windows; stage-1 ignition 60 min after release, about 150 s; stage 2 ignites about 140 s (PK34) / 2.5 min (PK30) after stage-1 burnout, burns about 108 s - [PK34], [PK30].

## STS-31, Discovery, Hubble Space Telescope
- Launch: 24 Apr 1990 12:33:51 UTC (8:33:51 EDT) - [WP31], [PK31 search summary]. [MR31] day 114 12:33:54.9 is main-engine start time, consistent. Kit's planned launch was 9:21 a.m. EDT 12 Apr (slipped) - [PK31].
- Orbit: planned 330 nmi circular, 28.45 deg - [PK31]. Actual about 613 x 615 km, 28.45 deg, apogee briefly 621 km - [WP31]. "380 miles (610 km)" - [AMS].
- Crew: Shriver (CDR), Bolden (PLT), Hawley, McCandless, Sullivan. Hawley to operate and release HST on the RMS; McCandless (EV1) and Sullivan (EV2) suited on standby; airlock depressurised to 5.0 psia, cabin lowered earlier - [PK31], [MR31]; [AMS] says Hawley lifted it out.
- Deploy timeline [MR31] (GMT day:hh:mm:ss, day 115 = 25 Apr): HST release (voice call) 115:19:37:51 = 25 Apr 19:37:51 UTC = MET 1d 07:04:00 (revolution 20). [AMS]: 3:37 p.m. EDT (matches). RCS separation burn 1 at 115:19:38:20 (29 s after release); burn 2 at 115:19:58:28. Orbiter then station-kept (about 40 nmi trail planned, 45 h standby) until the aperture door opened - [MR31], [PK31].
- Payload bay doors open: NOT FOUND (neither [MR31] nor [PK31]; a search summary quoted "12:27, 20:40, 24:50" with no unit or source page, not usable).
- RMS grapple and unberth times: NOT FOUND. [MR31]: RMS checkout and end-effector camera survey of HST done; grapple, unberth, deploy all normal. [PK31] plan: umbilical disconnect orbit 16, arrays orbits 17-18, high-gain antennas orbit 18, release orbit 19.
- Anomaly [MR31]: after unberthing, solar array panel 2 failed to deploy on attempt 1, partially deployed on attempt 2, deployed on attempt 3, then HST released; contingency EVA cancelled; all appendages (arrays, HGA, aperture door) deployed before release. Times of the three attempts: NOT FOUND.
- Hardware [PK31]: HST 43.5 ft (13.3 m) long, 14.0 ft (4.3 m) diameter arrays stowed, 40.0 ft (12.0 m) arrays deployed; array 7.9 x 39.9 ft (2.4 x 12.1 m) each, 2,400 W; mass 24,000 lb (11,000 kg); main mirror 94.5 in (2.4 m); aperture-door end pointed away from the Sun while on the arm.

## STS-34, Atlantis, Galileo (IUS-19)
- Launch: 18 Oct 1989 16:53:40.020 UTC (291:16:53:40.020 GMT; 11:53:40 p.m. CDT is the report's local label) - [MR34]; [WP34] 16:53:40 UTC. Inclination 34.30 deg [MR34].
- Orbit: planned 161 x 161 nmi after OMS-2, 177 x 161 nmi after deploy - [PK34]. [WP34] 298-307 km. Actual at deploy: NOT FOUND in [MR34].
- Crew: Williams (CDR), McCulley (PLT), Lucid, Chang-Diaz, Baker. Mission specialist who ran the deploy: NOT FOUND ([PK34] says deploy is done by mission specialists under orbiter crew command, generic).
- Timeline [MR34]: OMS-2 291:17:33:35.7 (140.8 s, 220 ft/s). Galileo deployment (voice call) 291:23:15:03 GMT = MET 6h 21m 23s ([PK34] plan 6:21:36; [WP34] 19:15 EDT). OMS-3 separation burn 291:23:30:02.2, 16.8 s, 31 ft/s (14 min 59 s after release). IUS burn (voice call) 292:00:15:15 GMT = MET 7h 21m 35s (1 h 00 m 12 s after release). Stage-2 ignition and payload-bay-doors-open times: NOT FOUND.
- Galileo: orbiter about 2 tons, probe 750 lb, about 5,200 lb with 2,400 lb propellant - [PK34]. Dimensions: NOT FOUND in [PK34].

## STS-30, Atlantis, Magellan (IUS)
- Launch: 4 May 1989 18:46:59.011 UTC (124:18:46:59.011 GMT) - [MR30]; [WP30] same.
- Orbit: OMS-2 124:19:31:26.28-19:33:31.88 [MR30]; kit plan 160 x 160 nmi, 176 x 161 post-deploy [PK30]; [MR30] actual after OMS-3: 178 x 159 nmi. CONFLICT: [WP30] 361 x 366 km (about 195-198 nmi) - not supported by [MR30]; do not use.
- Crew: Walker (CDR), Grabe (PLT), Thagard, Cleave, Lee. Who ran the deploy: NOT FOUND ([PK30] only says Lee and Cleave took part in a ground deployment exercise).
- Timeline [MR30]: Magellan deployed 125:01:01:32 GMT = MET 6h 14m 33s (kit plan 6:18 [PK30]); solar panels deployed afterwards on IUS command (about 10 min after release per [PK30]). OMS-3 separation 125:01:16:21.09-01:16:37.68 (16.4 s, 31.0 ft/s) = 14 m 49 s after release. IUS SRM-1 125:02:01:32 (MET 7:14:33; 1 h 00 m after release); SRM-1 separated, SRM-2 ignition 125:02:06:42 (MET 7:19:43). CONFLICT: [MAG] deploy 5 May 01:06:00 UTC vs [MR30] 01:01:32 UTC; use [MR30].
- Magellan: 6.4 m tall, 4.6 m diameter, high-gain antenna 3.7 m, launch mass 3,445 kg (dry 1,035 kg), Star 48B kick motor - [MAG] only (WP summary). Doors-open time: NOT FOUND.

## STS-41, Discovery, Ulysses (IUS + PAM-S)
- Launch: 6 Oct 1990 11:47:14.983 UTC (279:11:47:14.983 GMT) - [MR41]; [WP41] 11:47:15.
- Orbit: kit 160 x 160 nmi 28.45 deg, 160 x 177 after the planned OMS-3 (31 fps, 16 s) [PK41]. [MR41]: OMS-2 279:12:27:08.5; OMS-3 not listed in its table; a right-OMS 44 s, 41 ft/s maneuver at 280:10:43:53 gave 178 x 160 nmi (a day later, not a separation burn). [WP41] 300 x 307 km.
- Crew: Richards (CDR), Cabana (PLT), Melnick, Shepherd, Akers. Deploy "under the oversight of" Akers - [PK41].
- Timeline [MR41]: payload bay doors opened as planned (time NOT FOUND). Ulysses deployment (voice call) 279:17:48:14 GMT = MET 6h 00m 59s (kit plan 6:01:00). RCS separation burn 1 at 279:17:49:13 (59 s later); burn 2 not performed. IUS first burn (voice call) 279:18:53:13 = MET 7h 05m 58s (1 h 04 m 59 s after release). A thin arc-shaped debris piece filmed at the aft bay during deployment - [MR41]. PAM-S ignition time: NOT FOUND; search summary only: PAM separation from Ulysses at about 7 h 24 min MET.
- Spin-up to 70 rpm before PAM-S firing, 7 rpm after burn (yo-yo de-spun below 8 rpm, search summary), about 5 rpm after boom deployment [PK41]. Stack Ulysses/IUS/PAM-S in bay 44,024 lb [PK41].
- Ulysses: bus 10.5 x 10.8 x 6.9 ft, 5.4 ft dish, 807 lb, radial boom 18.2 ft, axial boom 26.2 ft, wire dipole 238 ft - [PK41]. PAM-S dimensions and mass: NOT FOUND (Star 48B motor, search/WP only).

## STS-37, Atlantis, Compton Gamma Ray Observatory (RMS release)
- Launch: 5 Apr 1991 14:22:44.988 UTC (095:14:22:44.988 GMT) - [MR37]; [WP37] 14:22:45.
- Orbit: planned circular about 243 nmi (after OMS-2 at 095:15:04:28) - [MR37], [PK37] 243 x 243; [WP37] 450-462 km; [CGRO] 450 km, 28.46 deg.
- Crew: Nagel (CDR), Cameron (PLT), Godwin, Ross (EV1), Apt (EV2). Who operated the RMS: NOT FOUND in [MR37]/[PK37].
- Timeline [MR37]: day 3 (097). RMS powered, grapple about 1 h after positioning the arm, move to release position about 1 h later; two solar-array relays deployed; GRO high-gain antenna would not deploy. Tried RCS shaking then RMS shaking, both failed. Contingency EVA (3 h 30 min per [MR37]) began; RMS lowered GRO so the antenna area was reachable from the bay; EV1 freed the antenna by shaking the boom laterally "early in the EVA"; GRO moved back to the release position. Release (voice call) 097:22:36:47 GMT = 7 Apr 22:36:47 UTC = MET 2d 08h 14m 02s, revolution 37, three revolutions late. Kit planned release MET 02/03:35:00 (02:03:35) [PK37]. EVA start clock time: NOT FOUND in [MR37]; [WP37] EVA 1 about 18:00-22:00 UTC, 4 h 32 min, EVA 2 on 8 Apr 14:51-20:38 UTC (5 h 57 min; [MR37] says 5 h 47 min). CONFLICT 3 h 30 vs 4 h 32 and 5 h 47 vs 5 h 57: unresolved. Separation burn after release: NOT FOUND ([MR37] OMS-3 at 099:15:19:13 is a later maneuver, 9.4 s). Doors-open time: NOT FOUND.
- Hardware: GRO 34,527 lb at the RMS ([MR37], heaviest payload the RMS had moved) vs 16,329 kg = 35,999 lb launch mass [CGRO]; about 5.5 m long, 4.5 m wide [CGRO]; 60 in (152 cm) high-gain antenna [PK37]. Four instruments: BATSE, OSSE, COMPTEL, EGRET.

## STS-93, Columbia, Chandra (IUS-27)
- Launch: 23 Jul 1999 04:30:59.984 UTC (204:04:30:59.984 GMT, 12:31 a.m. EDT) - [MR93]; [WP93] 04:31:00; [MCD] 0431 UTC. Planned 12:36 a.m. EDT 20 Jul [PK93].
- Scrubs: 20 Jul, mass spectrometer showed 640 ppm hydrogen in aft compartment at T-15 s, cause called "ion-pump-burp" [MR93]; [WP93] says T-7 s (conflict; use [MR93]). 22 Jul weather [WP93].
- Launch-day anomalies [MR93]: about 5 s after SSME start an LO2 post-deactivation pin loosened in SSME 3, struck the nozzle and ruptured three hydrogen cooling tubes (3.7 lb/s H2 leak); low-level LOX cutoff gave a 16 ft/s underspeed at MECO. About 5 s after liftoff a 0.5 s AC bus 1 short (damaged wire, port wire tray, bay 11/12) disqualified SSME 1 DCU A and SSME 3 DCU B; engines still ran to MECO. Orbit after OMS-2 (204:05:12:06.97, 135.8 s, 201.3 ft/s): 144.7 x 153.7 nmi.
- Crew: Collins (CDR, first woman Shuttle commander), Ashby (PLT), Coleman (MS1, deployer), Hawley (MS2, assists; deployed Hubble on STS-31), Tognini (MS3, backup) - [PK93], [MR93]. [PK93] states Collins is "the first woman to command a Space Shuttle mission".
- Timeline [MR93]: payload bay doors open 204:06:08:35 (MET 01:37:35; [MCD] 0610 UTC). Chandra/IUS deployed 204:11:47:01 GMT = MET 7h 16m 01s (kit plan 7:17; [MCD] 1147 UTC; tilt 29 deg planned MET 5:35, 58 deg then deploy [PK93]; [MCD] 58 deg at 1142 UTC). Orbiter -X separation 204:11:48:25 (84 s later): RCS F2F+F3F pulses of 0.54 s and 6.7 s. OMS-3 separation 204:12:02:25 (MET 7:31:25): left OMS 34.0 s, 34.5 ft/s, orbit 153 x 163 nmi. IUS stage 1 firing "about one hour after" deploy, two minutes; 2-min coast; stage 2 two minutes - [MR93]; [MCD] SRM-1 1248 UTC, cutoff 1250, SRM-2 burn 1251-1253, result 320 x 72,000 km, arrays 1322, IUS separation 1350 (search-page times to the minute). Kit plan: stage 1 125 s (19,621 lb propellant, 46,198 lbf), stage 2 117 s (16,350 lbf); transitional orbit 174 mi x 37,200 mi [PK93]; [MR93] achieved 327 x 72,067 km, then five IPS burns to final orbit (IPS-1 [MCD] 25 Jul 0111 UTC).
- Orbit at deploy: [MCD] 320 x 320 km (page times to the minute). [WP93] 260 x 280 km perigee/apogee: CONFLICT, [MR93] OMS-3 result is 153 x 163 nmi (283 x 302 km) and 144.7 x 153.7 before it; WP figure likely a rounding of the pre-burn orbit, not verified.
- Hardware [PK93]: Chandra 45.3 ft (13.8 m) long sunshade open, 38.7 ft closed; 64.0 ft wide arrays deployed, 14.0 ft stowed; 10,560 lb dry; 50,162 lb with IUS and ASE, "the heaviest payload ever launched on the Shuttle".

- Magellan 52 deg: a search summary of NASA's STS-30 material agrees with the kit (29 then 52 deg); not seen in the mission report.
