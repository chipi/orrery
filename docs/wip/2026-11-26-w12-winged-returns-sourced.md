## 1. STS-1 (Columbia), 14 Apr 1981, Edwards lakebed Runway 23

### Flown values
- Landing: 14 Apr 1981, 18:20:57 UTC (search summary only, no page opened states the seconds); [WS1] says "18:21 UTC". Mission elapsed time 2 d 6 h 20 m 53 s = 54:20:53 - [N40]. Runway 23, Rogers Dry Lake - [WS1], https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/sts-1-shuttle-columbia-touches-down-lakebed-runway-23-edwards-air-force-base (caption: "touches down on lakebed runway 23").
- Deorbit burn: two OMS engines, 160 s, over the Indian Ocean (southern Indian Ocean, 36th orbit per [WS1]) - [N40], [WS1]. A search summary says 155 s and 297 ft/s (search summary only, no page opened). Flown ignition MET and flown delta-v: NOT FOUND. (Plan values below.)
- Entry interface: 400,000 ft - [N40]; [WS1] says 120,000 m (390,000 ft), 8,110 km from the landing site, about 28,240 km/h. These two heights differ by 10,000 ft; [PK], [OFP], [WB] define EI as 400,000 ft (OFP plan value 406,000 ft). Flown EI time: NOT FOUND.
- Plasma blackout about 16 min; leading edges peak 2,750 F, under 600 F on the fuselage; comms back at Mach 10.3 - [N40].
- Roll reversals ("several", autopilot) - [N40]. [WS1] gives first roll about Mach 18.5, second about Mach 9.8, Mach 4.8 and Mach 2.8 reversals "automatically initiated and manually completed by John Young".
- Manual takeover (CSS): Mach 4.8, 112,000 ft; subsonic at 51,000 ft - [N40]. Post-flight slides: "first roll maneuver manually (CSS)" recommended for STS-2 - [SYM].
- Heading alignment: [WS1]/search summary say a 210-degree turn onto Runway 23 (search summary only). OFP plan: left-hand HAC (see below).
- Main-gear touchdown: 211 mph equivalent airspeed (339 km/h EAS), about 800 m (2,600 ft) further down the runway than planned - [WS1]; "211 miles per hour" - [N40]. [SYM] says "about 3100 ft long at 183 KTS EAS"; 183 kt = 211 mph, consistent. Main-gear touchdown 6,053 ft from threshold; long by 3,093 ft vs predicted (slide "3093 feet beyond predicted") - [SYM]. Conflict: 2,600 ft (WS1) vs 3,050/3,093/3,100 ft (SYM); SYM is the primary NASA source, WS1 is not.
- Nose-gear touchdown: 3,099 ft after main-gear touchdown; nose-gear sink 5.6 fps; pitchdown about 10 kt slower than planned - [SYM]. Nose-gear speed and time: NOT FOUND.
- Braking started 5,952 ft after main-gear touchdown; stopped after an 8,993 ft roll; wheels stopped 15,046 ft from the threshold - [SYM] (consistent: 6,053 + 8,993 = 15,046). 8,993 ft also in [N40].
- Rollout duration / wheel-stop clock time: a search summary says rollout "60 seconds" (search summary only; the [OFP] plan is 36 s, see below). Flown wheel-stop UTC: NOT FOUND.
- Speedbrake on the steep glideslope 68 percent vs 67.8 percent preflight - [SYM]. Pitch/yaw: lateral oscillation about Mach 1.6, 4 deg/s roll rate, 4 s period - [SYM].
- No drag chute on STS-1: the first drag chute on a Shuttle mission was STS-49 (Endeavour), landing 16 May 1992 at Edwards Runway 22 - https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/sts-49-endeavour-touchdown-edwards-with-first-drag-chute-landing/ ("first deployment of a drag chute on a shuttle mission"); "1:57 p.m. PDT, runway 22" (search summary only).

### Planned values (pre-flight; flown values differ, e.g. planned landing GET 54:27:43 vs flown 54:20:53)
- [PK] table: deorbit ignition GET 53:28:00, delta-V 299.0 fps (91 m/s), cutoff 53:30:27, orbit 149/2 n mi, entry interface 53:56:02, TAEM start 54:21:30, landing 54:27:43. Planned touchdown 185 kt (213 mph), sink 2.4 fps.
- [OFP] cycle 3: deorbit delta-V 292.9 fps, burn 2 min 28 s, free-fall 25 min 47 s to EI; EI 406,000 ft, 4,358 n mi range-to-go, 25,752 fps inertial, flightpath angle -1.21 deg, 191,902 lb; alpha 40 deg early, pitchover from 14,500 fps to 14.0 deg at TAEM interface (2,500 fps); crosswrange 700 n mi; wing leading-edge limit 2,663 F; reference peak heating rate figure garbled in OCR (NOT FOUND). TAEM: left-hand HAC, interface dynamic pressure 209 psf. A&L starts at 10,000 ft: 20-deg outer glideslope (was 22 deg), preflare to 1.5-deg inner glideslope, 473 fps EAS (280 KEAS, 265 psf) on the OGS, speedbrake retract 2,500 ft, preflare start 2,000 ft with 1.3 g or less, gear down at 270 KEAS (gear locked 11.8 s before touchdown), touchdown 2,942 ft past threshold at 185 KEAS (191 kt ground), 2.4 fps; speedbrake 50 percent at touchdown; planned rollout end 9,510 ft, 36 s after main-gear touchdown; derotation at -3 deg/s with weight on nose gear (range figure garbled in OCR, NOT FOUND).
- [PK] "Shuttle trajectory (final)" table: west lakebed edge Mach 1.2, 53,000 ft, 270 s to touchdown; east edge Mach 0.88, 44,000 ft, 230 s; abeam Boron heading 330 (turn final) 270 KEAS, 18,500 ft, 13,000 fpm, 20-deg glide path, 110 s; 10,000 ft 76 s; preflare 280 KEAS, 2,000 ft, 36 s; gear down command 270 KEAS, 250 ft, 19 s; gear locked 230 KEAS, 100 ft, 11 s; touchdown 180 KEAS, 0 s. (The table's "5 degrees glide path" beside gear-down is as printed; it conflicts with the 1.5-deg inner glideslope in [OFP]/[WB]; likely a typo, not resolved.)

## 2. Generic Shuttle entry profile
- Entry interface: 400,000 ft, typically 4,300 n mi from the runway - [WB]. Plan EI-to-TAEM times: closed-loop guidance 4:00 after EI; entry/TAEM interface 25:48; TAEM/A&L interface 30:16 at 300 KEAS - [WB] table 1-1.
- Alpha: preentry attitude hold at 40 deg; guidance holds 0 deg bank and 40 deg alpha until 0.132 g (3 ft/s2 drag) - [WB]. Pitchover reduces alpha to about 14 deg at Mach 2.5 - [OFP], [WB] nominal table (Mach 2.5: alpha 14, range 60 n mi, altitude 83 kft; Mach 2: alpha 13, 50 nm, 77 kft; Mach 1.5: alpha 10, 38 nm, 68 kft; Mach 1: alpha 8, 28 nm, 54 kft). Max-L/D alpha schedule (STS-59 flight card): Mach 3 alpha 17, Mach 2 alpha 15, Mach 1 alpha 12 - [WB].
- Bank: crossrange needs 37 deg bank above Mach 10 and 20 deg for the rest of entry; at Mach 11.5 lift must equal 75 percent of weight (equilibrium glide) - [WB].
- Drag limits: 2.5 g structural (Mach 16 to 4.5) - [WB].
- TAEM: starts when Earth-relative velocity falls under 2,500 ft/s (about Mach 2.5), about 60 n mi from threshold at about 82,000 ft AGL (text) / 83 kft (nominal table row) - [WB]. Conflict 82,000 vs 83,000 ft within the same document; also "85,000 ft" in a search summary (search summary only). HAC: overhead (turn 180-360 deg) or straight-in - [WB].
- A/L interface: altitude below 10,000 ft; outer glideslope 20 deg (weight under 222,000 lb) or 18 deg (heavier); preflare starts about 2,000 ft to the 1.5-deg inner glideslope; final flare about 80 ft - [WB]. STS-1 plan used 20 deg [OFP]. The brief's "19-20 deg" is not supported: the sources say 20 or 18 deg (STS-1 earlier plan was 22 deg).
- Guidance does not lower the gear, deploy the drag chute or brake - [WB].
- L/D: numeric hypersonic and subsonic L/D values: NOT FOUND in text (the workbook has L/D alpha lines and charts only). Orbiter shape "low L/D" - [WB].

## 4. Buran 1K1, 15 Nov 1988
- Launch: 06:00:02 Moscow time, Energia, Site 110, Baikonur - https://www.russianspaceweb.com/buran.html (page read; says "Two orbits or 206 minutes later, the Buran automatically lands at the Yubileiniy airfield at Site 251"). [WBU]: 03:00:02 UTC (same instant), pad 110/37, 2 orbits, 206 minutes, 83,707 km (the list-of-missions page https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Buran_missions says "3 hours, 25 minutes" and 6:00:00 Moscow time: 206 min = 3 h 26 min; "6:00:00" vs "06:00:02" differ by 2 s).
- Deorbit burn: ODU (combined propulsion system) engines "fired automatically to begin the descent" - [WBU]. Burn time, duration, delta-v, entry interface, heating, bank: NOT FOUND. russianspaceweb.com/buran-1k1.html was 404; https://www.buran-energia.com/blog/2013/11/15/25th-anniversary-of-the-burans-flight/ and https://buran.ru/htm/memory6.htm gave no landing detail (second one: certificate expired).
- Descent: 8,000 km through the atmosphere, slowed from 28,000 km/h to zero - [WBU].
- Approach and turn: at the 20 km key point the system, finding energy too high in gusty wind (15 m/s, gusts to 20 m/s), "performed additional turns in both directions and overflew the field to its northern side, before making a right turn back onto the final course" - [WBU]. The "other approach / other end of the runway" wording in the brief is NOT supported by this text (it describes extra turns and a right turn onto final, not a reversal to the opposite runway end). Which end Buran landed on: NOT FOUND (runway is 06/24, see section 5).
- Touchdown 06:24:42 UTC, stopped 06:25:24 (42 s, derived), within 3 m laterally and 10 m longitudinally of the target mark, crosswind 61.2 km/h (about 17 m/s) - [WBU]. Moscow time 09:25 = 06:25 UTC (search summary only, consistent).
- Touchdown speed: about 260 km/h (search summary only) vs 263 km/h (search summary only); rollout 1,620 m (search summary only). Not in any page opened: NOT FOUND (primary).
- Drag chute: search summary says the drogue chutes were meant to open on strut compression but opened 9 s later while rolling (search summary only, source unverified); chute deployment confirmed in no page opened. Nose-gear touchdown time: NOT FOUND.

## 5. Runways
- Edwards Rwy 23 (Rogers Dry Lake): [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwards_Air_Force_Base] gives the paved 05R/23L as 15,024 x 300 ft with "an extra 9,588 ft of lakebed runway at its northerly end"; that is the modern concrete runway, not the 1981 lakebed Runway 23. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogers_Dry_Lake lists lakebed runway "05/23 - 5.2 mi (8.4 km)" and "17/35 - 7.5 mi (12.1 km) primary", and notes 05/23 has "an extra 9,000 ft" beyond a 15,000 ft paved part (summary of fetched text; inconsistent with the Edwards page's 9,588 ft). Length of the 1981 lakebed Runway 23 itself and its width: NOT FOUND in a NASA source. [SYM] shows STS-1 stopped 15,046 ft from the threshold (so the strip is at least that long).
- KSC SLF: 15,000 ft (4,572 m) x 300 ft (91 m), designated 15/33 by direction, 1,001 ft paved overruns each end - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuttle_Landing_Facility . Magnetic heading figures (about 150/330): NOT FOUND (X-37B landed on 15 once and 33 twice per [WX]; the [PK] table has a Boron heading of 330 for Edwards, a different thing).
- Vandenberg: runway 12/30, 15,000 ft (4,572 m) concrete; extended from 8,500 ft to 15,000 ft for Shuttle - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandenberg_Space_Force_Base . Width: NOT FOUND.
- Yubileyniy (Site 251): runway 06/24, 4,500 x 84 m, M-600 concrete 26-32 cm - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yubileyniy_Airport ; same 4,500 m x 84 m in https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baikonur_Cosmodrome (that page's fetch summary also called the orientation "north-south", which is wrong for a 06/24 runway; ignore).
