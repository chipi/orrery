## Decisions for the operator

1. **Where the Roadster mission lives.** No destination fits a heliocentric orbit with no target.
   Options: (a) `missions/sun/` beside Parker / Solar Orbiter; (b) `missions/mars/` as a Mars-crossing
   flight, which it is not (it never targeted Mars); (c) a new `heliocentric` group. Recommend (a).
2. **Booster return, full frame for Falcon Heavy (UXD-052).** Today FH side boosters stay in the inset.
   The demo flight earns the full frame: both side boosters landing side by side at LZ-1 and LZ-2, the
   centre core missing the drone ship. Recommend: full frame only for missions whose sourced timeline
   has both side-booster landings (falcon-heavy-demo, roman); every other FH flight keeps the inset.
   Roman's side boosters landed at LZ-2 and LZ-40 (NASA); its centre core's fate is a search summary only.
3. **Timeline precision.** Only the pre-launch planned timeline was found for the demo (SpaceX press kit
   and Spaceflight Now agree); actual landing times and the third-burn time were not. Use the planned
   METs, labelled as planned, or hold W13 until actual times are found.
4. **Brand marks.** The model draws none. If the operator wants the red car to read as a Tesla, a
   text-free silhouette is as far as it goes without a logo rule.
5. **Roman mass** is unresolved (Wikipedia 9,174 kg looks like a lb/kg mix-up; a NASA snippet says
   4,166 kg). The card shows no mass until a NASA page states it.
