## Station configuration on the day

- ISS, 2 Nov 2000: Zarya, Unity (PMA-1, PMA-2), Zvezda, plus Z1 truss and PMA-3 (STS-92, ISS-3A, launched day 285 23:17:00Z = 11 Oct 2000 (mine), landed 298:20:59:42Z = 24 Oct 2000 (mine) [SUM92]; payload column reads "ISS Z1 TRUSS, PMA-3"). Next assembly flight STS-97 (ISS 4A) launched 336:03:06:01Z = 1 Dec 2000 (mine, day-of-year) and carried the P6 truss per its designation 4A (payload column not read) [SUM97]; so P6 was NOT yet on orbit on 2 Nov 2000. Progress M1-3 had been at the aft port and undocked 1 Nov 2000 07:04:49 Moscow time [RSW-TM31] (the port was empty for TM-31). Zvezda zenith/nadir occupancy: NOT FOUND. The NASA ISS page says only that the station "consisted primarily of the Zvezda Service Module" as the docking point, which is not a module list (https://www.nasa.gov/?p=168679).
- Mir, 15 Mar 1986: base block alone (launched 19 Feb 1986). Kvant arrived 1987; Progress 25 arrived 21 Mar 1986 (aft). Forward port Kurs, aft port Igla [RP1357; RSW-T15].
- Salyut 7, 6 May 1986: Salyut 7 docked with Cosmos 1686 at the other end ("Salyut-7-Kosmos-1686 stack", WP via search summary; RP1357 has a "Cosmos 1686 and Salyut 7" figure 3-7).
- Mir, 29 Jun 1995: base block, Kvant (1987), Kvant 2 (1989), Kristall (1990) per RP-1357 (state as of Nov 1994, no Spektr), plus Spektr (named in [MR71]: Spektr solar panel fully deployed, TORU boxes carried on Spektr) [MR71 lines 405-408, 842-846]. Spektr arrival date: NOT FOUND (not in opened sources). Priroda was not yet launched (1996; not in sources either). Mir arrays were in a "non-symmetric configuration" [MR71 line 2462]. Soyuz TM-21 (Mir-18 crew's) was docked; it was moved for a free-flight after the Shuttle left and redocked early after a Mir attitude-control loss [MR71 lines 877-890]. Which Mir port Soyuz TM-21 and Progress used: NOT FOUND.
- Tiangong, 17 Jun 2021: Tianhe + Tianzhou-2 (above). Wentian and Mengtian not yet launched (Jul and Oct 2022 per shortlist WP).
- ISS, 31 May 2020: section 2.

## Conflicts (side by side)

- STS-71 capture time: [MR71] 180:13:00:13.9 GMT at two places, but its own MET (01:17:27:18.9 at line 419, 01:18:27:18.9 at line 1226) with launch 19:32:18.988 gives 12:59:38 (mine) or 13:59:38 (mine); [SUM71] MET 1/17:27:57 = 13:00:16 (mine) and text 13:00:14Z. Use 13:00:14 UTC, uncertainty a few seconds.
- STS-71 launch seconds: 19:32:18.988 [MR71] vs 19:32:18.95 [SUM71].
- STS-71 [SUM71] "crews transfer time 180:16:08:18Z" vs docking complete 13:08:18; probably a typo; not used.
- STS-71 undocking: "Soyuz undocking 6/15:32:34 MET" in [SUM71] is the Soyuz free-flight, different from the Orbiter undocking 185:11:09:42.
- Demo-2 soft capture: 10:16 am EDT [NASA-D2, AMER]; a search summary gave 10:10 am (not opened, rejected).
- Demo-2 hatch opening: NASA blog "approximately 12:45 pm EDT" (scheduled) vs 1:02 pm EDT (search summary).
- Demo-2 mission length: the NASA release summary line "approximately 3 months" is wrong against its own dates (30 May to 2 Aug, about 2 months); trust the dates.
- Demo-2 planned capture 10:29 EDT [EDA] vs actual 10:16 (13 min, mine) vs "16-17 min" in the shortlist.
- Shenzhou 12 docking date: [SFN-SZ12] says "June 16"; People's Daily and UTC give 17 Jun (07:54 UTC). 16 Jun is the EDT date of the launch only.
- Shenzhou 12 duration: 90 days (plan, shortlist) vs "92 days in orbit" [SFN-SZ12].
- TM-31 docking second: 09:21:03 (WP) vs 09:21:40 [RSW-TM31] (Moscow 12:21:40, mine to UTC).
- Soyuz T-15 final Mir undock: 16 Jul 09:09:50 UTC [SF-T15] vs "undocked from Mir on July 5" (search summary of WP-based text), with landing on 16 Jul; the 5 Jul figure is probably the Salyut-bound undock error; unresolved.
- Soyuz T-15 duration: 124 d [RP1357] vs 125 d [RSW-T15].
- Kristall length: 13.73 m (RP-1357 OCR) vs 11.9 m (search summary); OCR may misread digits; confirm in the PDF image before use.
- Kristall APAS node location: RP-1357 says "At aft end is a node with two ports" vs a garbled line saying "the node on the lateral end of the Kristall module"; the first reading is clean OCR, the second damaged.
- Mir page of WP "March 1987" for T-15 launch is wrong (shortlist).
