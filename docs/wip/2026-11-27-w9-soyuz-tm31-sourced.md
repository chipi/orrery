## Conflicts

- Docking time: 09:21:03 UTC ([WP-TM31], [SF], [WEE]) against 09:21:40 UTC ([RSW], [ISSCOM]). Possibly contact vs hooks, but no source says so. 37 s apart. Use 09:21:03 for contact, treat 09:21:40 as the alternative; this is my choice, not sourced.
- Hatch: 10:23 UTC ([ESA], [ASTRO], [AMSP]) against "90 minutes after docking" ([WP-E1] as returned) and "4:23 a.m." (search summary).
- Local times: [AMSP] writes "EDT" for 4:21/5:23 a.m., but 2 Nov 2000 was EST (UTC-5), which gives 09:21 and 10:23 UTC; the "EDT" label is wrong, 4:21 does equal 09:21 UTC at UTC-5 (mine). Baikonur times in the same source imply UTC+5.
- Landing site: 90 km NE of Arkalyk ([WP-TM31]), 82 km NE ([WEE]), 46 km NW of Arkalyk ([SF32]) with coordinates 50 38'42" N 66 43'54" E ([SF]). The coordinates sit north of Arkalyk (about 50.25 N, 66.9 E from memory, not opened), so the 46 km NW wording fits less well than "NE"; unresolved.
- Soyuz TM-31 final undock: 02:21:09 UTC on 6 May ([WP-TM31]) against "05.05.2002, 00:31:08 UTC" ([SF32], a typo, year 2002, date 5 May).
- Orbit at docking: 379 x 388 km ([OF]) against 374-391 and 384-396 (mission-wide, [SF], [WEE]).
- Zvezda max diameter 4.35 / 4.2 / 4.15 / 4.1 m; Zvezda mass 20,320 kg vs 24.6 t.
- Soyuz TM length 7.48 m ([WP-TM]) against 7 m ([RSW-TM]) against 6.98 m (search summary only).
- Soyuz TM-31 insertion orbit: 185 x 231 km ([OF]) against 182 x 233 ([AMSP]) against 190 x 249 ([WP-TM31]).
