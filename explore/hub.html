Every world here is a place you can actually go — built from real orbital data, close enough to look around. Start with the whole solar system in motion, or drop straight onto a surface.

  • OUR SOLAR SYSTEM The solar system in 3D Spin, zoom, click any planet for live orbital elements.
  • Stellar Neighborhood The real nearby-star field, in 3D. Fly out past the Sun into the real HYG catalogue — thousands of nearby stars, named and placed in 3D.
  • Milky Way Our galaxy, as a labelled schematic. Pull back into the galaxy: the spiral arms, the bulge, and Sagittarius A* at the core.
  • Local Group The Milky Way's galaxy neighbourhood. One step further out — Andromeda, Triangulum, the Magellanic Clouds, and dozens of dwarf galaxies.
  • EARTH Earth orbit explorer Active satellites, GPS, ISS, JWST — everything in our backyard.
  • MOON Moon surface map Every Apollo landing site, every Soviet rover, every active orbital asset.
  • MARS Mars surface map 27 missions plotted on a real DEM — Curiosity's path, Perseverance's cache, the lot.
