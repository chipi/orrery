Mission target

Mars

Earth–Mars launch windows open every 26 months — driven by orbital resonance. Miss the window and wait two years.

The highest eccentricity of the inner planets (e=0.093) — Mars swings ~20% farther from the Sun at aphelion than at perihelion, driving big seasonal and dust-storm swings. Launch windows, by contrast, are set by the ~26-month Earth–Mars synodic period.

SCIENCE

  • Axial Tilt and Seasons Mars's 25.2° axial tilt is remarkably close to Earth's — but combined with its eccentricity, southern summers are intensely hot and short while northern summers are long and mild. Why Earth has seasons, why Uranus rolled onto its side, and why Venus spins backward.
  • Eccentricity Mars's 0.0935 eccentricity (six times Earth's) gives a 20 % swing in solar flux per orbit — the engine behind global dust storms that can engulf the planet for months. How stretched the orbit is — zero is a perfect circle, one is a parabolic escape.
  • Hohmann Transfer Mars launch windows open every 26 months — the synodic period between Mars and Earth — and Hohmann transfer is why every Mars mission departs in one of those windows. The cheapest two-burn trip between two coplanar circular orbits — and the baseline mission planners measure everything else against.
  • Moons of the System Mars has two small captured-looking moons — Phobos (orbiting below synchronous altitude, spiraling inward) and Deimos (slowly receding). Galileo's four-night observation in 1610 ended geocentrism. The system's moons are still ending old assumptions today.
  • Active Spacecraft Survey Mars hosts the densest active spacecraft census beyond Earth — orbiters from five agencies and surface rovers / landers from three. What's at each planet right now: from MRO at Mars to Juno at Jupiter — while Venus has had no orbiter since Akatsuki ended in 2025.

MISSIONS

  • Mariner 4 / 6 / 7 / 9 — NASA, 1965–72 (first flybys then first Mars orbiter, global mapping)
  • Viking 1 + 2 — NASA, 1976 (first successful Mars landings; biology experiments)
  • Mars Pathfinder + Sojourner — NASA, 1997 (first wheeled rover on another planet)
  • Mars Global Surveyor + Odyssey + Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter — NASA, 1997– (multi-decade orbital coverage; MRO HiRise)
  • Mars Express + ExoMars TGO — ESA, 2003– / 2016– (mineralogy, methane search)
  • Spirit + Opportunity — NASA, 2004 (twin rovers; Opportunity lasted 14 years)
  • Phoenix — NASA, 2008 (north polar lander; first to touch water ice)
  • Curiosity / MSL — NASA, 2012– (Gale crater; nuclear-powered rover, still operating)
  • MAVEN — NASA, 2014– (atmospheric escape physics)
  • Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) — ISRO, 2014–22 (India's first interplanetary mission)
  • InSight — NASA, 2018–22 (first interior-structure mission; marsquakes)
  • Hope (Al Amal) — UAE, 2021– (atmospheric weather monitoring)
  • Tianwen-1 + Zhurong — CNSA, 2021 (China's first orbital + rover combination)
  • Perseverance + Ingenuity — NASA, 2021– (Jezero crater; first powered flight on another planet)
  • ExoMars Rosalind Franklin — ESA, scheduled 2028 launch
