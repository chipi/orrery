Space Shuttle Atlantis

OV-104 — fourth Shuttle. Flew final mission STS-135 (2011), final Hubble servicing STS-125 (2009), deployed Galileo + Magellan

Atlantis, OV-104, flew the bookends of the Shuttle's greatest partnerships. It made the most dockings with the Russian station Mir, opening the era of US-Russian cooperation, and it flew STS-125, the final, daring servicing mission that left the Hubble Space Telescope good for another decade. In July 2011 Atlantis flew STS-135, the 135th and last Shuttle mission — the flight that closed thirty years of reusable American spaceflight and left the Space Station it had helped build in orbit above.

Fourth operational orbiter; first flight STS-51-J (1985-10-03) was a classified DoD mission. Highlights: deployed Magellan (STS-30, 1989) and Galileo (STS-34, 1989) — the two planetary probes that opened Venus and Jupiter to long-duration observation; flew 7 of the 9 Shuttle-Mir docking missions between 1995-1997; flew the final Hubble servicing mission STS-125 (2009-05) — the only post-Columbia non-ISS-rendezvous mission approved; flew STS-135 (2011-07) — the final Shuttle flight ever. On display at Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex since 2013.

CREW

  • STS-51-J (1985-10-03) · first Atlantis flight; classified DoD mission (DSCS-III deployment) —
  • STS-30 (1989-05-04) · deployed Magellan probe to Venus — first US planetary mission in 11 years —
  • STS-34 (1989-10-18) · deployed Galileo probe to Jupiter via VEEGA trajectory —
  • STS-71 (1995-06-27) · first Shuttle-Mir docking; first US-Russian crew handover; 100th US crewed spaceflight —
  • STS-125 (2009-05-11) · final Hubble servicing mission (HST SM4); only post-Columbia mission without ISS rendezvous —
  • STS-135 (2011-07-08) · final Shuttle flight ever; delivered Raffaello MPLM to ISS; closed the 30-year Shuttle programme —

Crewed

  • Space Shuttle Discovery OV-103 — third Shuttle. Deployed Hubble (STS-31, 1990); flew both return-to-flight missions. 39 missions — most of any orbiter
  • Space Shuttle Challenger OV-099 — second operational Shuttle. Lost 73s after launch on STS-51-L (1986-01-28) with all seven crew including teacher Christa McAuliffe
  • Space Shuttle Columbia OV-102 — the first Shuttle orbiter to fly to space (STS-1, 1981-04-12); lost on re-entry STS-107 2003-02-01
  • Space Shuttle Orbiter Reusable winged orbital crew vehicle
  • Space Shuttle Endeavour OV-105 — fifth Shuttle, built from spares to replace Challenger. First Hubble servicing STS-61 (1993); delivered Unity + Canadarm2
  • Space Shuttle Enterprise OV-101 — prototype Shuttle. Renamed from Constitution after Star Trek fan campaign. Flew 1977 atmospheric tests; never reached orbit
