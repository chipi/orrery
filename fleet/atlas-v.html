Atlas V

United Launch Alliance's evolved Atlas

The reliable workhorse of United Launch Alliance, with over 100 successful launches since 2002. Carried Curiosity, Perseverance, MAVEN, MRO, New Horizons, and many of the most consequential US planetary missions of the 21st century.

BUILDER
United Launch Alliance
COUNTRY

2002

1981-2011
Epoch
ISS Assembly

MISSIONS

  • New Horizons 2006 First flyby of Pluto and its moons; sharpest images of a Kuiper belt world
  • Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter 2009 Highest-resolution lunar maps ever made
  • X-37B OTV-1 (USA-212) 2010 USA-212 — 224 days on orbit, Vandenberg AFB
  • Curiosity 2011 First nuclear-powered Mars rover; first sky-crane landing
  • Juno 2011 First polar orbiter of Jupiter; solar-powered beyond the asteroid belt for the first time
  • X-37B OTV-2 (USA-226) 2011 USA-226 — 469 days on orbit, Vandenberg AFB
  • X-37B OTV-3 (USA-240) 2012 USA-240 — 675 days on orbit, Vandenberg AFB
  • MAVEN 2013 First mission dedicated to studying how Mars lost its atmosphere
  • X-37B OTV-4 (USA-261, AFSPC-5) 2015 USA-261 / AFSPC-5 — 718 days on orbit, KSC Shuttle Landing Facility
  • OSIRIS-REx 2016 Largest asteroid sample ever returned (121.6 g of Bennu, delivered to Earth 2023-09-24)
  • InSight 2018 First to detect marsquakes; first interplanetary launch from US West Coast
  • Perseverance 2020 First helicopter on another planet (Ingenuity); first sample caching on Mars
  • X-37B OTV-6 (USA-299, USSF-7) 2020 USA-299 / USSF-7 — 908 days on orbit, KSC Shuttle Landing Facility
  • Solar Orbiter 2020 First mission designed to image the Sun's polar regions in detail; complements Parker Solar Probe's in-corona measurements with high-latitude remote sensing
  • Lucy 2021 First mission to the Jupiter Trojans; visits 11 bodies across 8 asteroids and their satellites in a single 12-year trajectory — a record number of objects on separate solar orbits

Engines

  • RL10 Hydrogen upper stage, six decades and counting
  • RD-180 Russian ox-rich staged combustion the US could not replicate

Fleet

  • X-37B OTV USSF reusable robotic spaceplane — multi-year classified orbital missions, the only US spaceplane operational after the Shuttle's retirement
  • Cape Canaveral SLC-41 Atlas V's main pad — flew every Atlas V mission including Mars Perseverance + Lucy + Starliner CFT
  • Curiosity (MSL) Nuclear-powered Mars Science Laboratory rover
  • Juno Polar Jupiter orbiter; magnetosphere studies
  • OSIRIS-REx Sample-returned Bennu (2023); now en route to Apophis
  • InSight Mars seismometer; 1300+ marsquakes detected
  • Perseverance (Mars 2020) Carried Ingenuity; Mars sample caching
  • Solar Orbiter First detailed imaging of the Sun's polar regions
  • Lucy First mission to the Jupiter Trojans

Launcher

  • Delta II NASA's medium-lift planetary-science workhorse — 155 flights 1989-2018
  • SLS Block 1 Heavy lift for Artemis lunar program
  • Space Shuttle stack Orbiter + ET + SRBs; 135 missions
  • Atlas SLV-3D Centaur 1970s NASA outer-planet workhorse — Pioneer 10/11, Helios 1/2, Mariner 9/10, Pioneer Venus
  • Saturn V Sent humans to the Moon
  • Saturn IB Lifted Apollo 7, Skylab crews, and ASTP
