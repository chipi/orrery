ATV (Automated Transfer Vehicle)

ESA's flagship ISS cargo carrier; 5 flights

The Automated Transfer Vehicle was Europe's ISS freighter and the most sophisticated cargo craft of its time — a twenty-tonne spacecraft that navigated itself to a fully automatic docking with the Station, delivered cargo, water, air and propellant, and used its own engines to reboost the entire ISS in orbit. Five flew between 2008 and 2015, each named for a European thinker from Jules Verne to Georges Lemaître. More than a supply truck, the ATV was how Europe earned its place as a full ISS partner — and the technology it proved lives on as the European Service Module that now powers NASA's Orion on its way to the Moon.

ATV (Automated Transfer Vehicle) is a robotic cargo spacecraft built by Astrium / Airbus in Europe. ESA's flagship ISS cargo carrier that flew five missions to the ISS from 2008 to 2015, delivering cargo, water, air and propellant while using its engines to reboost the Station.
