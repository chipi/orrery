Baikonur Site 200

Proton pad — the Mir core, Salyut 7, Nauka, and probes from Venera to ExoMars

Baikonur Site 200 has two Proton pads, 200/39 and 200/40. Pad 39 launched the core of the Mir space station in 1986 and its Kvant-1, Kvant-2 and Kristall modules, and in 2021 the Nauka module for the ISS; pad 40 launched Salyut 7. Planetary probes left from both pads: Venera 13 to 16, the Vega 1 and 2 missions to Venus and Halley's Comet, Fobos 1 and 2, the failed Mars-96, and the 2016 ExoMars Trace Gas Orbiter. Pad 40 has stood idle since it was set aside for an Angara rebuild that moved to another site; pad 39 still flies Proton-M, including commercial launches for International Launch Services.
