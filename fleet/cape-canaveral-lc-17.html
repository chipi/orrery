Cape Canaveral Launch Complex 17 (LC-17)

Thor and Delta pad pair: Spirit, Opportunity, Pathfinder, MESSENGER, Dawn; demolished in 2018

Two Thor-lineage pads, 17A and 17B, near the beach at Cape Canaveral. The Air Force built them in 1956 for the Thor missile, then upgraded them in the early 1960s for Thor-derived rockets, which became the Delta family. Pioneer 0, an attempted lunar orbiter, was lost here on 17 August 1958 when its Thor-Able exploded 73.6 seconds after liftoff. In the Delta II years the pads sent NASA probes to Mars and beyond: NEAR Shoemaker and Mars Pathfinder in 1996, Spirit and Opportunity in 2003, MESSENGER in 2004, Phoenix and Dawn in 2007. The Delta III flew three times from 17B between 1998 and 2000 before the programme was abandoned. The last launch was GRAIL on 10 September 2011, and both towers were demolished on 12 July 2018.

MISSIONS

  • NEAR Shoemaker 1996 First spacecraft to orbit an asteroid (433 Eros, 2000) and first soft landing on any small body (2001-02-12)
  • Mars Pathfinder 1996 First wheels on Mars; demonstrated airbag landing
  • Spirit 2003 First of the Mars Exploration Rover twins; longest-lived rover until passed by Opportunity
  • Opportunity 2003 Held the Mars driving record (45.16 km) until Perseverance passed it in October 2026; 14-year surface lifetime against a 90-sol design
  • MESSENGER 2004 First spacecraft to orbit Mercury; first complete surface map of the innermost planet
  • Phoenix 2007 First confirmation of water ice in the Martian arctic regolith; first soft propulsive Mars landing since Viking 2
  • Dawn 2007 First mission to orbit two extraterrestrial bodies — Vesta, then Ceres

Fleet

  • Delta II NASA's medium-lift planetary-science workhorse — 155 flights 1989-2018
  • Sojourner First Mars rover; Pathfinder mission
  • Spirit (MER-A) Mars Exploration Rover; 6-year operation
  • Opportunity (MER-B) Twin to Spirit; nearly 15-year Mars operation
  • MESSENGER First Mercury orbiter — 6 planetary gravity assists over 6.6 years to bleed energy before insertion in 2011
  • Phoenix Confirmed water ice in Martian soil
  • Dawn Only spacecraft to orbit two extraterrestrial worlds — Vesta then Ceres via ion propulsion

Launch site

  • Launch Complex 14 (LC-14) Cape Canaveral Mercury-Atlas pad — launched all 4 orbital Mercury flights (Glenn / Carpenter / Schirra / Cooper, 1962-1963)
  • Launch Complex 5 (LC-5) Cape Canaveral Mercury-Redstone pad — launched Freedom 7 (Shepard, 1961-05-05) and Liberty Bell 7 (Grissom, 1961-07-21)
  • Baikonur Site 1/5 (Gagarin's Start) Sputnik 1 (1957). Vostok 1 — Gagarin (1961). Five hundred and forty Soyuz launches from a single pad.
  • Baikonur Site 31/6 Active Soyuz crewed pad — every ISS Soyuz crew rotation since 2020 has lifted from Site 31
  • Launch Complex 34 (LC-34) NASA pad at Cape Canaveral (1961-68) — first Saturn I; Apollo 1 fire site 1967-01-27; Apollo 7 first crewed launch 1968-10-11
  • Vandenberg SLC-4E Polar / sun-synchronous gateway — Falcon 9 to Starlink + national-security + earth-observation
