Crew Dragon

First commercial human-rated orbital spacecraft

SpaceX’s human-rated spacecraft, the first privately developed and operated spacecraft to carry humans to orbit (Demo-2, 2020-05-30). The trunk features two solar array panels that deploy after fairing separation for power during orbital operations.
