Hayabusa

First asteroid sample ever returned to Earth

JAXA's pioneering asteroid sample-return mission. Launched 2003-05-09 to Itokawa carrying four μ10 microwave-discharge ion thrusters. Survived a hydrazine leak, three of four reaction wheels failing, an ion engine failure, and seven weeks of lost communications before engineers nursed it back to Earth with one working engine assembled from the surviving halves of two damaged ones. Sample capsule recovered at Woomera AUS 2010-06-13 with 1500 μg of Itokawa material — the first asteroid sample ever returned to Earth.
