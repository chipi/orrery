Kourou ELA-2

Early Ariane pad — 1 Ariane 2, 2 Ariane 3, 116 Ariane 4 (1986-2003)

ELA-2 (Ensemble de Lancement Ariane 2) at Kourou's Centre Spatial Guyanais hosted 119 Ariane launches: one Ariane 2 (1986-03-28), two Ariane 3, and all 116 launches of the Ariane 4 rocket (1988-2003). Its near-equatorial position gave Ariane 4 a substantial GTO performance advantage over launch sites at higher latitudes, anchoring Arianespace's commercial dominance in the 1990s. Decommissioned after Ariane 4 retired; ELA-3 (Ariane 5) and ELA-4 (Ariane 6) are its successors.
