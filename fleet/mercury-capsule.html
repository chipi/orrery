Mercury capsule

First U.S. crewed spaceflight (Shepard)

The Mercury capsule was designed to be the smallest thing that could keep a person alive in space — and it shows. There was no room to do much but sit; the astronaut wore the spacecraft as much as rode it. But its shape solved the era's hardest unsolved problem — how to come back. The blunt end, coated in a shield built to char and carry the heat away, turned reentry from a fatal certainty into something survivable, and its cone was the first American answer to the question of what a crewed spacecraft even looks like. Every capsule since — Gemini, Apollo, Dragon — is a descendant of this cramped little cone.

The cone-shaped capsule that carried six American astronauts on Project Mercury between 1961 and 1963. Tiny enough that John Glenn famously described being "spam in a can" — but it proved orbital flight was achievable for the United States.

MISSIONS

  • Freedom 7 / MR-3 1961 First American in space
  • Liberty Bell 7 / MR-4 1961 Second American suborbital flight; hatch malfunction lost the capsule (recovered 1999)
  • Friendship 7 / MA-6 1962 First American to orbit the Earth
  • Aurora 7 / MA-7 1962 Repeated Glenn's 3-orbit profile; overshot landing target by 400 km
  • Sigma 7 / MA-8 1962 First textbook Mercury flight — 6 orbits; double Glenn / Carpenter duration
  • Faith 7 / MA-9 1963 Last solo American orbital flight; 22 orbits / 34 hours

Fleet

  • Mercury-Redstone First US human-rated launcher — variant of the Army's Redstone IRBM. Launched Freedom 7 (1961-05-05) and Liberty Bell 7 (1961-07-21)
  • Launch Complex 5 (LC-5) Cape Canaveral Mercury-Redstone pad — launched Freedom 7 (Shepard, 1961-05-05) and Liberty Bell 7 (Grissom, 1961-07-21)
  • Atlas LV-3B Lifted Mercury astronauts to orbit
  • Launch Complex 14 (LC-14) Cape Canaveral Mercury-Atlas pad — launched all 4 orbital Mercury flights (Glenn / Carpenter / Schirra / Cooper, 1962-1963)

Crewed

  • Gemini Two-person crew; rendezvous and EVA practice
  • Apollo CSM (Block I) Original Apollo CSM design — never flew crewed; killed Apollo 1 crew on the pad 1967-01-27. Redesign produced the Block II flown 1968-1975
  • Apollo CSM Block II Lunar-capable Command + Service Module
  • Apollo Lunar Module First crewed lunar lander; Eagle, Antares, Falcon...
  • Space Shuttle Enterprise OV-101 — prototype Shuttle. Renamed from Constitution after Star Trek fan campaign. Flew 1977 atmospheric tests; never reached orbit
  • Space Shuttle Columbia OV-102 — the first Shuttle orbiter to fly to space (STS-1, 1981-04-12); lost on re-entry STS-107 2003-02-01

You can see this in

  • Griffin Museum of Science and Industry, Chicago — Aurora 7, flown by Scott Carpenter (1962)
  • Space Center Houston, Houston — Faith 7, flown by Gordon Cooper (1963)
  • Cosmosphere, Hutchinson, Kansas — Liberty Bell 7, flown by Gus Grissom (1961) and raised from the Atlantic in 1999
  • Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex, Merritt Island, Florida — Sigma 7, flown by Wally Schirra (1962)
  • National Air and Space Museum, Washington — Friendship 7, flown by John Glenn on the first American orbital flight (1962)
