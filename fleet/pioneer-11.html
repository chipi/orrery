Pioneer 11

First spacecraft to encounter Saturn — Jupiter flyby in 1974 then gravity-assist to Saturn in 1979

Spin-stabilised RTG-powered probe (~259 kg), sibling of Pioneer 10 with the same bus and Pioneer Plaque message-to-the-stars. Launched 1973-04-06 on an Atlas SLV-3D Centaur D-1A + Star-37E from Cape Canaveral. Jupiter flyby 1974-12-03 at 43 000 km — the closest of any Pioneer encounter, returned the first polar-region images and the gravity assist needed to reach Saturn. Saturn encounter 1979-09-01 at 21 000 km from the cloud tops — first detailed images of the ring system, discovery of a new ring (F-ring) and a new moon, confirmation of Saturn's nearly-aligned magnetic field. Continued outbound observations until last contact 1995-11-24. Now ~93 AU from the Sun, headed toward Aquila.

MISSIONS

  • Pioneer 11 1973 First spacecraft to fly past Saturn (1979-09-01); demonstrated the gravity-assist that sent Voyager 2 onward

Fleet

  • Atlas SLV-3D Centaur 1970s NASA outer-planet workhorse — Pioneer 10/11, Helios 1/2, Mariner 9/10, Pioneer Venus
  • Cape Canaveral Launch Complex 36B (LC-36B) USAF Atlas-Centaur pad — Mariner 10, Pioneer 11 (1973), Pioneer Venus (1978); now Blue Origin New Glenn (leased 2015, flew 2025)

Orbiter

  • Pioneer 10 First spacecraft to traverse the asteroid belt
  • Mariner 9 First spacecraft to orbit another planet
  • Viking 1 Orbiter Companion orbiter to Viking-1 lander — relay + 50000 surface images at 150-300 m/pixel, the first global Mars map. Operated 1976-1980
  • Voyager 1 Most distant human-made object
  • Voyager 2 Only spacecraft to visit all four giant planets
  • Lunar Orbiter 3 Third US lunar orbiter (1967-02-08). Photographed Surveyor 1 sitting on the lunar surface — first photo of a spacecraft on another world
