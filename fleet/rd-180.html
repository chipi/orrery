RD-180

Russian ox-rich staged combustion the US could not replicate

The RD-180 represents a branch of propulsion technology the United States effectively ceded to the Soviet Union during the Cold War. Oxygen-rich staged combustion — burning excess oxidiser through the preburner to drive the turbopump — demands materials and manufacturing tolerances that American industry had not mastered. NPO Energomash's two-chamber, single-turbopump design flew on Atlas V from May 2000, providing the first stage with a combination of thrust and efficiency that no American-made kerolox engine of the era could match. It was retired with Atlas V in 2023.
