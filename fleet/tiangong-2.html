Tiangong-2

Second Chinese station testbed

Tiangong-2 is a space station built by CAST in China. Second Chinese station testbed. Launched in September 2016, hosted Shenzhou 11 (30-day mission, October–November 2016, China's longest crewed mission at the time) and Tianzhou-1 cargo ship (2017); deorbited July 19, 2019.
