Ascent to orbit

Δvneeded=vorbit+ℓgrav+ℓdrag+ℓsteer\Delta v_{\text{needed}} = v_{\text{orbit}} + \ell_{\text{grav}} + \ell_{\text{drag}} + \ell_{\text{steer}}Δvneeded​=vorbit​+ℓgrav​+ℓdrag​+ℓsteer​

The climb's full bill: Δv_needed = v_orbit + ℓ_grav + ℓ_drag + ℓ_steer. Each ℓ is speed the rocket burns without keeping — the gap between orbit's sticker price and what ascent really costs.

Parameters

Payload to orbit [kg]
100 (100 – 20,000)
Target orbit altitude [km]
200 (150 – 500)

Results

Gravity loss
0.822 km/s
Drag loss
0.04095 km/s
Steering loss
0.2488 km/s
Final speed
7.773 km/s

Assumes: Planar (2-D) ascent, eastward launch, single-exponential atmosphere · Exponential atmosphere (constant scale height)
