Booster count

Nb=⌈(Freq−Fcore)/Fb⌉N_b = \lceil (F_{\text{req}} - F_{\text{core}})/F_b \rceilNb​=⌈(Freq​−Fcore​)/Fb​⌉

N_b = ⌈(F_req − F_core)/F_b⌉: whatever thrust the core stage can't provide, strap-on boosters must — each adds F_b. Round up, because half a booster is not a thing.

Parameters

Gross liftoff mass [kg]
500,000 (100 – 4,000,000)
Core thrust [N]
1,000,000 (0 – 40,000,000)
Thrust per booster [N]
4,000,000 (100,000 – 16,000,000)
Liftoff thrust-to-weight
1.3 (1 – 3)

Results

Boosters needed
2

Assumes: Launch from Earth (g₀ = 9.81 m/s²) · Evaluated at liftoff (full mass)
