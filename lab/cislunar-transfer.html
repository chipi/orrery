Trans-lunar transfer (Earth→Moon)

a=12(rLEO+rMoon),tof=πa3/μ⊕a = \tfrac12(r_{LEO}+r_{Moon}),\quad t_{of} = \pi\sqrt{a^3/\mu_\oplus}a=21​(rLEO​+rMoon​),tof​=πa3/μ⊕​​

A half-ellipse to the Moon: a = ½(r_LEO + r_Moon), flight time t_of = π√(a³/μ⊕). The same Hohmann geometry as the planet transfers — the Moon is just close enough to make it days, not months.
