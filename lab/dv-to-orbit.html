Δv to Orbit

vreq=vorbit+vlossv_{\text{req}} = v_{\text{orbit}} + v_{\text{loss}}vreq​=vorbit​+vloss​

The real price of orbit: v_req = v_orbit + v_loss — the orbital speed itself plus everything the climb steals (gravity and drag). Orbit is a speed, and you must buy more than the sticker price.
