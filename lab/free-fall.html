Free Fall

t=2hg,v=2ght=\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}},\quad v=\sqrt{2gh}t=g2h​​,v=2gh​

Drop from height h and gravity does the rest: fall time t = √(2h/g), impact speed v = √(2gh). No thrust, no drag — gravity alone, the baseline every descent card fights against.

Parameters

Drop height [m]
100 (0.01 – 1,000,000)
World
Earth · Moon · Mars · Venus · Mercury

Results

Fall time
4.516 s
Impact speed
44.29 m/s

Assumes: No air drag · Uniform gravity
