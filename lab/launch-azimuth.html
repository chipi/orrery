Launch azimuth

cos⁡A=cos⁡i/cos⁡φ\cos A = \cos i / \cos\varphicosA=cosi/cosφ

cos A = cos i / cos φ: to reach inclination i from latitude φ, aim at azimuth A. You cannot launch to an inclination below your latitude — the equation itself refuses (|cos A| ≤ 1).

Parameters

Launch-site latitude [deg]
28.5 (0 – 90)
Target inclination [deg]
51.6 (0 – 145)

Results

Compass heading to launch
45.02 deg

Assumes: Non-rotating Earth (ignores the launch-site velocity turn) · Spherical Earth
