Staging

Nstages=⌈Δv/(veln⁡(1/ε))⌉N_{\text{stages}} = \lceil \Delta v / (v_e\ln(1/\varepsilon)) \rceilNstages​=⌈Δv/(ve​ln(1/ε))⌉

One stage tops out at vₑ·ln(1/ε) — even carrying nothing, the structure fraction ε caps its Δv. Divide the mission Δv by that ceiling and you get the number of stages you must drop along the way.

Parameters

Total Δv to reach [km/s]
9.4 (1 – 20)
Engine efficiency (Isp) [s]
350 (150 – 470)
Structural fraction ε
0.08 (0.02 – 0.25)

Results

Single-stage Δv ceiling
8.669 km/s
Stages needed
2

Assumes: Ideal staging (ignores carrying the upper stages) · Equal stages
