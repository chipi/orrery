Touchdown bounce

vb=e vtd,h=(1R−vb22μ)−1−Rv_b = e\,v_{td},\quad h = \left(\tfrac{1}{R} - \tfrac{v_b^2}{2\mu}\right)^{-1} - Rvb​=evtd​,h=(R1​−2μvb2​​)−1−R

Touch down at v_td and the surface gives back v_b = e·v_td (restitution e). In micro-gravity (this goal's opening card) that bounce can arc kilometres high — Philae's double bounce, as an equation.

Body

Assumes: Point mass · Uses the body's mean radius — real small worlds are lumpy, and local gravity varies across their surface. · Vertical rebound along the local radius; rotation and sideways motion are ignored.
