Curiosity

Gale Crater (Aeolis Mons / Mt. Sharp)

Mars Science Laboratory. RTG-powered (no solar reliance), car-sized (899 kg), still operational over a decade after landing. Confirmed Gale Crater hosted a habitable lake environment in the deep past. Climbing Mt. Sharp's stratigraphic sequence — billion-year-by-billion-year readings of Mars's environmental history.

First nuclear-powered Mars rover; first to confirm a past habitable environment on Mars.

NASA's Curiosity rover launched November 26, 2011 atop an Atlas V from Cape Canaveral. After 254 days of cruise, Mars Science Laboratory delivered the 899-kg nuclear-powered rover to Gale Crater on August 6, 2012 via the now-famous sky-crane landing. More than a decade later Curiosity is still climbing Mount Sharp, drilling rocks that hold a record of Mars's once-habitable past.
