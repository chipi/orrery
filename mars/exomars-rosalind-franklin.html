Rosalind Franklin rover

Oxia Planum (target)

ESA's first Mars rover. Originally a Russian-led joint mission planned for 2022; cancelled after the 2022 invasion of Ukraine and rescoped as ESA-led with NASA contributions. Re-targeted for 2028 launch, 2029 arrival. Carries a 2-metre subsurface drill — the deepest yet planned for a Mars rover, designed to reach below the radiation-damaged top metre.

Will reach 2 m subsurface depth; the first directly-targeted astrobiology mission since Viking.

Rosalind Franklin is Europe's first Mars rover, built to look for signs of past life. Its drill can reach 2 m below the surface, deeper than any rover before it, where organic molecules are shielded from the radiation that breaks them down near the top. The rover was ready to fly in 2022 on a joint mission with Russia; that partnership ended after Russia invaded Ukraine, and ESA rebuilt the mission with NASA. It is planned to launch in 2028 and land at Oxia Planum, a plain of clay-rich rock laid down in water about four billion years ago.
