Mars Pathfinder + Sojourner

Ares Vallis

First-ever Mars rover (Sojourner, 11 kg, microwave-sized). Demonstrated airbag-cushioned EDL — the same pattern Spirit and Opportunity later used. Sojourner traversed ~100 m and analysed 16 rock and soil targets before the lander relay died. Pathfinder's images popularised Mars on the early web — the first interplanetary mission whose data the public consumed in real time.

Proved low-cost, faster-cheaper Mars missions could deliver primary science. Inaugurated mobile robotic exploration of Mars.

Mars Pathfinder landed in Ares Vallis on July 4, 1997 — the first US mission to Mars since the Vikings 21 years earlier. The lander deployed Sojourner, the first wheels on Mars, demonstrating airbag-landing + roving in a $265M low-cost "faster-better-cheaper" mission that returned ~16,500 images. Sojourner's 100-meter traverse mapped twelve rocks named after cartoon characters (Yogi, Barnacle Bill, Scooby Doo) before the lander died.
