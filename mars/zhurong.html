Zhurong (with Tianwen-1 lander)

Utopia Planitia

China's first Mars rover. Operated 347 sols, traversed 1.92 km. Returned ground-penetrating radar evidence for buried subsurface layers consistent with ancient catastrophic flooding events. Lost contact during the 2022 Martian winter; CNSA confirmed no further communications.

First non-US rover to operate on Mars; first non-US GPR-equipped Martian rover.

Zhurong landed in Utopia Planitia on May 14, 2021 — China's first successful Mars landing and the first Mars mission by any nation to combine orbiter, lander, and rover in a single launch (Tianwen-1). Named after the Chinese god of fire, Zhurong drove 1.92 km across the southern Utopia plains before being put into hibernation in May 2022 during dust season. CNSA has not confirmed wake-up.
