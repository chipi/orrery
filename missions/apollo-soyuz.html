Apollo-Soyuz Test Project (ASTP)

First international human spaceflight cooperation; first US-USSR rendezvous + docking

Apollo-Soyuz is where the space race ends — not at a finish line, but with a handshake. In July 1975 an American Apollo and a Soviet Soyuz, the rival spacecraft of the rival superpowers, met and docked in orbit, and Tom Stafford and Alexei Leonov shook hands through the open hatch between them. It took a jointly-built docking adapter, two crews learning each other's language, and a thaw in the Cold War deep enough to let the era's fiercest competitors cooperate in full view of the world. Nothing about it was strictly necessary; that was the point. After eighteen years of racing to be first, the two programs chose, for one flight, simply to meet — and set the template for the international cooperation that would one day build the Space Station.

The Apollo-Soyuz Test Project — first international crewed spaceflight, first US-USSR rendezvous + docking, and the final flight of an Apollo CSM. Apollo (Stafford + Brand + Slayton) launched 1975-07-15 from LC-39B; Soyuz 19 (Leonov + Kubasov) launched the same day from Baikonur. Apollo's docking module (built by NASA + Rockwell) used a new androgynous docking ring (APAS) that allowed either spacecraft to be active — the precursor of every international docking system since (Mir, ISS, Tiangong, Dragon, Starliner). Hatch opening 1975-07-17 ~16:00 UTC; the crews exchanged flags, gifts, and joint experiments across 2 dockings (initial July 17, practice July 19) totalling ~44 hours docked. Soyuz returned 1975-07-21; Apollo continued 5 more days in orbit before splashdown 1975-07-24 — last Apollo splashdown ever. CSM-111 is on display at the California Science Center, LA (alongside Endeavour). The détente-era cooperation that ASTP modelled informed the Shuttle-Mir programme 20 years later.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH (Apollo) — Saturn IB (AS-210) from LC-39B 1975-07-15 19:50 UTC. 7.5 h after Soyuz 19 (Baikonur 1975-07-15 12:20 UTC).
  2. DOCKING — Apollo + Soyuz 19 dock 1975-07-17 16:09 UTC. First US-USSR rendezvous + dock. Hatch opens 19:17 UTC; Stafford–Leonov handshake ~3 hours later (~22:19 UTC).
  3. JOINT OPS — Flag + gift exchange; joint experiments; 2 dockings across ~44 hours docked.
  4. UNDOCK — Final undock 1975-07-19 16:26 UTC. Soyuz 19 lands 1975-07-21 in Kazakhstan.
  5. SPLASHDOWN (Apollo) — Apollo splashed down in Pacific 1975-07-24 21:18 UTC — last Apollo splashdown ever, and the last US crewed water landing until SpaceX Crew Dragon in 2020. CSM-111 displayed at California Science Center LA.
