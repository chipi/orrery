Apollo 14

Third lunar landing — Apollo 13's lost target reached; Shepard's golf shots

Apollo 14 had to prove the program could still land. It flew nine months after Apollo 13 nearly killed its crew, took over the target Apollo 13 never reached, and put Alan Shepard back in space a decade after he became the first American to leave the planet — grounded in between for years by an inner-ear disorder, and now, at forty-seven, the oldest person to walk on the Moon. He and Mitchell came up about thirty metres short of Cone Crater's rim, unable to tell in the confusing terrain how close they had actually gotten. Then, in the last minutes on the surface, Shepard swung a makeshift six-iron at two golf balls — a stunt, and also a quiet signal that the crisis was behind them and Apollo was flying again.

The third crewed Moon landing and Alan Shepard's second flight — making him both the first American in space (Freedom 7, 1961) and the fifth man on the Moon, at age 47 the oldest Moonwalker. Apollo 14 was the recovery mission after Apollo 13's aborted landing, taking over Apollo 13's intended Fra Mauro Highlands target. Shepard and Mitchell deployed ALSEP, completed two EVAs totalling 9 h 23 m, and pulled the modular equipment transporter (MET, a rickshaw-style cart — the LRV was still one mission away) toward Cone Crater, though they came up ~30 m short of the rim due to navigation uncertainty in the cratered terrain. In the final minutes of EVA-2, Shepard improvised a six-iron golf-club head onto a sample-collection handle and hit two golf balls "miles and miles and miles" (one was later analysed from imagery at ~22 m and ~37 m). Stuart Roosa carried tree seeds in Kitty Hawk that became the "Moon Trees" planted across the US. 42 kg of samples returned.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Saturn V (AS-509) from Kennedy Space Center LC-39A 1971-01-31 21:03 UTC.
  2. TLI — Trans-lunar injection. S-IVB ∆v ~3.05 km/s.
  3. CSM-LM DOCKING — Six docking attempts (the first five failed) before Kitty Hawk soft-captured Antares — anomaly in docking latches; root cause never reproduced on ground.
  4. LOI — Lunar orbit insertion. SPS ∆v ~0.88 km/s.
  5. TOUCHDOWN — Antares lands at Fra Mauro Highlands 1971-02-05 09:18 UTC — within 53 m of target despite an abort-program glitch in the LM computer that the team patched in real-time.
  6. EVA-1 — Shepard + Mitchell deploy ALSEP + first lunar geology traverse on this mission.
  7. CONE CRATER + GOLF — EVA-2: traverse toward Cone Crater (came up ~30 m short of rim). Final minutes — Shepard hits two golf balls with an improvised club.
  8. ASCENT — Antares lifts off after 33 h 31 m on surface. 42 kg of samples returned.
  9. TEI — Trans-Earth injection. SPS ∆v ~1.0 km/s.
  10. SPLASHDOWN — CM splashed down in South Pacific 1971-02-09 21:05 UTC. Recovery by USS New Orleans. Last Apollo quarantine.
