BepiColombo

First Europe + Japan joint planetary mission; second-ever spacecraft to orbit Mercury (after Messenger)

AGENCY
European Space Agency (Mercury Planetary Orbiter + Mercury Transfer Module) / Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (Mercury Magnetospheric Orbiter, Mio)
DEPARTURE
2018-10-20
ARRIVAL
2026-11-21
TRANSIT
2954 days
∆V
~7.2 km/s (ion-cruise + 9 gravity assists + MOI)
VEHICLE
Ariane 5 ECA
PAYLOAD
4100 kg launch stack: MTM (Mercury Transfer Module, ESA — 4 ion thrusters, solar arrays) + MPO (Mercury Planetary Orbiter, ESA, 1230 kg, 11 instruments) + MIO (Mercury Magnetospheric Orbiter, JAXA, 255 kg, 5 instruments) + MOSIF sunshield. Two science orbiters separate after Mercury arrival.

Joint ESA / JAXA mission to Mercury, launched 2018-10-20 — named after Italian mathematician Giuseppe (Bepi) Colombo, who designed Mariner 10's multi-flyby trajectory. The 8-year cruise uses 9 gravity assists (Earth 2020, Venus 2020, Venus 2021, then six Mercury flybys 2021–2025) plus continuous ion-electric thrust from the Mercury Transfer Module to bleed off the enormous solar-gravity gradient between Earth and Mercury orbits. Mercury orbit insertion 2026-11-21; science operations begin early 2027 after MTM jettison and separation of the two orbiters — ESA's MPO (planetary surface + composition) and JAXA's Mio (magnetosphere). Tests of general relativity via radio-science range tracking through Mercury's strong solar-gravity environment are also planned.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Ariane 5 ECA lifts BepiColombo from Kourou on its 8-year ion-cruise to Mercury.
  2. EARTH FLYBY — Single Earth gravity assist begins the inner-system gravity-assist chain.
  3. VENUS FLYBY #1 — First Venus gravity assist.
  4. VENUS FLYBY #2 — Second Venus gravity assist begins the Mercury approach phase.
  5. MERCURY FLYBY #1 — First of six Mercury gravity assists.
  6. MERCURY ORBIT INSERTION — MTM jettison + orbiter separation; science operations begin early 2027.
