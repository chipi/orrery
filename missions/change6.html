Chang'e 6

First sample return from the lunar far side

AGENCY
China National Space Administration / China Academy of Space Technology
DEPARTURE
2024-05-03
ARRIVAL
2024-06-01
TRANSIT
30 days
∆V
~5 km/s
VEHICLE
Long March 5
PAYLOAD
8200 kg launch mass; 1.935 kg sample returned

Combines the architectures of Chang'e 4 (far-side communications) and Chang'e 5 (sample return). The Queqiao-2 relay satellite was launched first (March 2024) to support the mission. Chang'e 6 then collected 1.935 kg from Apollo Crater on the far side — material with very different composition from any lunar sample previously returned. The Moon's two hemispheres are radically different (the far side has thicker crust, fewer mare); these samples may finally explain why.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Long March 5 from Wenchang.
  2. LOI — Lunar orbit insertion.
  3. TOUCHDOWN — Lander descends to Apollo Crater (far side) 2024-06-01 (UTC). All comms via Queqiao-2 relay.
  4. SAMPLING — 1.935 kg collected from far-side regolith.
  5. ASCENT — Ascent vehicle lifts off; rendezvous with orbiter.
  6. EARTH RETURN — Capsule lands in Inner Mongolia 2024-06-25 with the first far-side samples ever recovered.
