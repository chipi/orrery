Dawn

First mission to orbit two extraterrestrial bodies — Vesta, then Ceres

AGENCY
National Aeronautics and Space Administration / Jet Propulsion Laboratory
DEPARTURE
2007-09-27
ARRIVAL
2015-03-06
TRANSIT
2717 days
∆V
~11 km/s (approx)
VEHICLE
Delta II 7925H
PAYLOAD
Ion-propelled orbiter; framing camera, VIR, GRaND, etc.

Dawn proved ion propulsion at planetary scale: it matched orbits with massive Vesta, left, and spiralled down onto dwarf planet Ceres — the largest object in the asteroid belt. Bright spots in Occator crater became one of the mission's iconic mysteries, later tied to briny eruptions from a deep reservoir. This catalogue entry uses Ceres as the primary destination; Vesta is covered in the narrative above.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Delta II lifts Dawn toward Vesta and Ceres on a low-thrust spiral highway.
  2. CERES CAPTURE — Ion engines brake Dawn into orbit around Ceres; mapping campaign begins.
