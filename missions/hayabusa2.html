Hayabusa2

Second asteroid sample return (Ryugu, 2020); first artificial impact crater on an asteroid; first subsurface asteroid material returned to Earth

AGENCY
Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (lead) — DLR + CNES (MASCOT lander) + NASA (DSN tracking, sample-curation partnership)
DEPARTURE
2014-12-03
ARRIVAL
2018-06-27
TRANSIT
1302 days
∆V
~2 km/s (ion engine cumulative)
VEHICLE
H-IIA 202
PAYLOAD
490 kg dry, ion-electric propulsion (4×μ10 thrusters) + chemical bipropellant + 1.6 m HGA. Onboard payload — Small Carry-on Impactor (SCI, 2.5 kg shaped charge), MASCOT lander (10 kg, DLR/CNES), 3× MINERVA-II rovers (~1 kg each), sample return capsule (16 kg, parachute reentry).

JAXA's follow-on to the original Hayabusa. Launched 2014-12-03, Hayabusa2 reached carbonaceous near-Earth asteroid (162173) Ryugu on 2018-06-27 after a 3.5-year low-thrust ion cruise + Earth gravity assist. Surface operations 2018–2019: two touchdown-and-go sample collections bracketing a Small Carry-on Impactor experiment (2019-04-05) that excavated a 14 m crater for the second sample. Three MINERVA-II rovers returned the first surface images from an asteroid; the DLR/CNES MASCOT lander operated for 17 hours. Sample return capsule reentry over Woomera on 2020-12-05 (UTC; recovered 2020-12-06 local/JST) delivered 5.4 g of pristine Ryugu material — the first asteroid sample with subsurface stratigraphy. Extended mission: flyby of 98943 Torifune (2001 CC21) in 2026-07; rendezvous with fast-rotating (1998 KY26) targeted for 2031-07.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — H-IIA 202 from Tanegashima begins the 3.5-year cruise to asteroid Ryugu.
  2. EARTH FLYBY — Earth gravity assist phases the rendezvous orbit.
  3. RYUGU ARRIVAL — Hover at 20 km — start of 1.5-year proximity operations including sample collection.
  4. EARTH RETURN — Sample capsule lands in Woomera, Australia with 5.4 g of Ryugu material.
  5. TORIFUNE FLYBY — Extended-mission flyby of 98943 Torifune (2001 CC21).
  6. 1998 KY26 RENDEZVOUS — Planned rendezvous with fast-rotating asteroid (1998 KY26).
