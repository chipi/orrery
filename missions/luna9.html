Luna 9

First soft-landing on the Moon; first photographs from the lunar surface

AGENCY
Soviet Academy of Sciences (continued by Roscosmos)
DEPARTURE
1966-01-31
ARRIVAL
1966-02-03
TRANSIT
3 days
∆V
~3 km/s
VEHICLE
Molniya-M
PAYLOAD
1583 kg launch mass; 99 kg lander capsule

After 11 Soviet failures and 5 American Ranger missions designed only to crash, Luna 9 became the first spacecraft to soft-land on another world. Nine images — including five panoramas — returned across seven radio sessions (8 h 5 min in total) over three days settled a long-running scientific debate: the Moon's surface was solid, not a sea of dust deep enough to swallow a lander. The mission lasted 3 days before battery depletion. Within months, every Moon-bound mission afterwards used soft-landing as the baseline.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Molniya-M from Baikonur 1966-01-31.
  2. TOUCHDOWN — Soft-landing at Oceanus Procellarum 1966-02-03 18:45 UTC. First soft-landing on another world.
  3. FIRST IMAGES — Nine images including five panoramas, returned in seven transmission sessions (8 h 5 min in total) over three days.
  4. SIGNAL LOSS — Battery depleted; final transmission 1966-02-06.
