Mangalyaan

First Asian Mars mission; first Mars success on first attempt

AGENCY
Indian Space Research Organisation
DEPARTURE
2013-11-05
ARRIVAL
2014-09-24
TRANSIT
323 days
∆V
~3.5 km/s
VEHICLE
PSLV-XL (C25)
PAYLOAD
1337 kg

ISRO's Mars Orbiter Mission cost $74 million — less than the budget of the film Gravity ($100M), and a fraction of NASA's MAVEN ($671M) which arrived two days earlier. Built in 18 months as a technology demonstrator, Mangalyaan succeeded at every objective: trans-Mars injection, deep-space navigation, Mars orbit insertion, and 8 years of science return. India became the fourth space agency at Mars and the first to succeed on its first try.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — PSLV-XL (C25) from Sriharikota — a relatively small launcher requiring orbit-raising loop.
  2. PERIGEE BURN 1 — First of six Earth orbit raising burns before trans-Mars injection.
  3. TRANS-MARS INJECTION — Final perigee burn places spacecraft on Mars trajectory.
  4. MARS ORBIT INSERTION — Liquid Apogee Motor fires after ~10 months dormant — fired for 24 minutes to capture into orbit.
  5. MISSION END — Lost contact 2022-04 after the propellant ran out and the battery drained below its safe limit during long eclipses (ISRO).
