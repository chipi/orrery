OSIRIS-REx

Largest asteroid sample ever returned (121.6 g of Bennu, delivered to Earth 2023-09-24)

AGENCY
National Aeronautics and Space Administration / Goddard Space Flight Center / University of Arizona / Lockheed Martin
DEPARTURE
2016-09-08
ARRIVAL
2018-12-03
TRANSIT
816 days
∆V
~1.4 km/s
VEHICLE
Atlas V 411
PAYLOAD
2110 kg launch mass (880 kg dry)

OSIRIS-REx carried out one of the most exacting asteroid-proximity operations ever attempted. Launched 2016-09-08 from Cape Canaveral on an Atlas V 411, used a 2017 Earth gravity assist to rotate its orbital plane onto Bennu's 6° inclination, and rendezvoused with the 492-m carbonaceous near-Earth asteroid 2018-12-03. After stationkeeping at ~19 km, it later entered a record orbit 680 m above the surface (930 m from Bennu's centre, June 2019) — the closest spacecraft orbit ever around any celestial body. Spent two years mapping Bennu's boulder-strewn surface and discovering active particle ejections. 2020-10-20 the Touch-and-Go sample arm descended to the Nightingale site, fired N₂ gas into the surface, and grabbed a plume — sinking 50 cm into a 'rubble-pile fluid' surface. Sample Return Capsule delivered 121.6 g of Bennu material to Utah 2023-09-24. The spacecraft itself was immediately redesignated OSIRIS-APEX, en route to a 2029 Apophis rendezvous.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Atlas V 411 from Cape Canaveral SLC-41.
  2. EARTH GRAVITY ASSIST — 2017-09-22, 17,237 km over Antarctica — rotates orbital plane onto Bennu's 6° inclination.
  3. BENNU RENDEZVOUS — Arrives at Bennu; stationkeeps at ~19 km then progressively tightens to a record orbit 680 m above the surface (930 m from Bennu's centre, June 2019) — closest spacecraft orbit ever around any celestial body.
  4. TAG SAMPLE COLLECTION — 2020-10-20. Touch-and-Go arm sinks 50 cm into the rubble-pile surface; grabs 121.6 g.
  5. BENNU DEPARTURE — 2021-05-10. 2.5-year cruise back to Earth.
  6. SAMPLE RETURN — 2023-09-24. SRC parachutes into Utah Test and Training Range; spacecraft becomes OSIRIS-APEX.
