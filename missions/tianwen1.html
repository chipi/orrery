Tianwen-1

First mission to successfully deliver orbiter, lander, and rover to Mars in a single launch

AGENCY
China National Space Administration / China Aerospace Science and Technology Corp.
DEPARTURE
2020-07-23
ARRIVAL
2021-02-10
TRANSIT
202 days
∆V
~5 km/s
VEHICLE
Long March 5
PAYLOAD
5000 kg (orbiter + lander + Zhurong rover)

China's first Mars mission was the first to succeed, in one launch, at what only the Soviet Mars 3 had attempted in 1971: orbital insertion, atmospheric entry, soft landing, and rover deployment. The Zhurong rover operated 347 sols (358 Earth days) at Utopia Planitia, traversing 1.9 km, before entering planned hibernation in May 2022; it has not woken since (likely due to dust accumulation on solar panels). The orbiter remains active, mapping the planet at high resolution and serving as relay for ground assets.

EVENTS

  1. LAUNCH — Long March 5 from Wenchang — China's first interplanetary launch, on a heavy-lift rocket.
  2. MARS ORBIT INSERTION — Engine burn captures spacecraft into Mars orbit.
  3. ZHURONG LANDING — Lander/rover descends to Utopia Planitia 2021-05-14; Zhurong rolls off ramp 2021-05-22.
  4. ZHURONG HIBERNATION — Rover enters planned dormancy 2022-05; orbiter continues operations.
