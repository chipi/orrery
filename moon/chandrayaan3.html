Chandrayaan-3

Shiv Shakti Point (south polar region)

First spacecraft to land that close to the lunar south pole, at about 69°S — the most contested territory in current space exploration, where permanently shadowed craters harbour water ice. Pragyan made the first in-situ detection of sulfur near the lunar south pole using its LIBS instrument. India became the fourth nation to soft-land on the Moon.

Vikram lander · Pragyan rover

Polar landing technique — Mars's south pole region (where seasonal water ice exists) becomes a destination only after the polar landing problem is solved on the Moon.

Chandrayaan-3 landed near the lunar south pole on August 23, 2023 — India's first soft landing on the Moon and the closest landing to the south pole by any nation. The Vikram lander deployed the Pragyan rover, which drove ~100 m across 14 days searching for water ice in permanently shadowed terrain. India became the fourth nation to soft-land on the Moon, after USSR, USA, and China.

  3. Surface — Shiv Shakti Point, near lunar south pole — landed August 23, 2023
