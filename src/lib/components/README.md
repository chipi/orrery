# src/lib/components — module map

Svelte components shared across routes, by family. Scene-specific ones live with their scene (`surface-scene/`, `lab/`); pure logic lives in `$lib/orbital`, `$lib/universe`, `$lib/surface-map`.

- **Root-layout shell** — `Nav`, `CommandPalette`, `DebugPanel`, `AudioOverlay`, `LanguageSuggestion`, `ExhibitOverlay`. `SensorySheet` is the unified settings sheet Nav opens; `LocalePicker` also sits in Nav. In `Nav`, every right-hand control wears the shared `.nav-btn` box (32 px fine pointer, 44 px touch; per-toggle rules carry state colours only), and High contrast / Share / Debug are one `moreItems` list rendered as the More menu (`#nav-more-menu`, outside the `<nav>`) and as phone drawer rows ([UXD-016](../../../docs/uxs/decisions/UXD-016-compact-nav-and-more-menu.md)).
- **Detail panels** — `Panel.svelte` is the shell (right drawer, mobile bottom sheet; props `grabFocus`, `zIndex`, `mobileSheet`, `onShare`, `onBack`, `covered`, and the `eyebrow` / `heading` snippets: a mission opened over a body panel sets `onBack` on its bar and `covered` on the body panel, UXD-019). It owns the one fixed header (`.panel-head`, [UXD-018](../../../docs/uxs/decisions/UXD-018-panel-shell-and-actions.md) as amended by B1.1): line 1 is the `eyebrow` snippet (the kind line) with back, Share (when `onShare` is set) and close right-aligned on the same line, all `Chip`s; line 2 is the `heading` snippet (the name) at full panel width. A panel passes its kind line and its name as those two snippets (each guarded by its own `{#if entity}`, since Panel renders them even while the entity is null), keeps key stats and the rest in the scrolling content, and never draws its own close; with no `eyebrow` line 1 carries the actions alone. `PanelActions` is the one action row (at most three `Chip`s under the identity block, above the hero; order in-scene, view, simulate, plan); no panel stacks CTAs at its foot. Content panels wrap it: `PlanetPanel`, `SmallBodyPanel`, `SatellitePanel`, `SunPanel`, `BeltPanel`, `StarPanel`, `MissionPanel`, `FleetEntryPanel`, `StationModulePanel`, `PhasePanel`, `RegimePanel`, and the /explore-scale `MilkyWay` / `LocalGroup` / `LocalSheet` / `Virgo` / `Laniakea` / `CosmicWeb` / `DeepSky` / `Exoplanet` / `BlackHole` panels. Parts: `PanelTabRow`, `PanelHeroImage`, `PanelLightbox` (full-screen gallery viewer: pass `images={gallery}` and it steps through them), `AgencyRow`, `ScienceCard` (one `/science` section as a card; a panel lists at most five of them, picked through `$lib/science-links`: `pickScienceLinks` caps and dedupes, `rankMissionScience` ranks a mission's picks by class and defining phase, `refsFromSlugs` reads `tab/section` slugs; no default list, UXD-022), `ScienceChip` (the per-field `?` glyph, outside the cap).
- **Star, outer-scale and black-hole panels** — `UniversePanelBody` is the planet skeleton for `StarPanel`, `DeepSkyPanel`, `BlackHolePanel` and `MilkyWayPanel` / `LocalGroupPanel` / `LocalSheetPanel` / `VirgoPanel` / `LaniakeaPanel` / `CosmicWebPanel` (it renders `Panel` itself: props `kind` or `eyebrow`, `name`, `hero` or `heroOf` {level, id}, `heroArt`, `gallery`, extra `tabs` + `tabContent`, `lead`, `facts`, `extra`, `science`, `learn`, `actions`); `UniverseFacts` is its labelled value cells (also the star's TECHNICAL tab). A panel on it carries no private head, cell or `rem` rule (`scripts/check-universe-panels.sh`, [UXD-023](../../../docs/uxs/decisions/UXD-023-star-and-outer-panels-follow-the-planet-pattern.md))
- **Named `*Panel` but NOT on the shell** — `ConicSectionPanel` (/fly overlay), `ScienceLayersPanel` (Science Lens), `SiteStoryPanel` (surface site story). `EarthObjectPanel`, `MuseumPanel`, `ObservatoryPanel` live in `surface-scene/`.
- **/fly** — `LaunchScene` / `CoastScene` / `DescentScene` (the flight acts; math in `$lib/orbital`), `LaunchTelemetry`, `AscentLosses`, `SpacecraftInfoCard`, `FlightDirectorBanner`; `PhaseMarkerLabel` (cislunar markers) vs `FdPhaseMarkerLabel` (the four FlightDirector stages).
- **/explore v2** — `ScaleIndex` (the INDEX of each of the eight scales, one component, look in `src/lib/styles/index-panel.css`, UXD-017), `StarPortrait`, `ConstellationFinder`, `HrDiagram`, `MassPeriodChart`, `CausalityMap`, `ExploreScalePicker` (`align` left or right: the ladder's screen edge), `ScaleBar` (the one scale bar: a constant-width bar and its label; Explore and both Worlds bars, UXD-021), `SizesCanvas` (the eight planets' bodies at true relative size; names are DOM in `SizesOverlay`).
- **Chip** — `Chip` (toggle / choice / action / link; the one control for every toggle, filter, choice and HUD action), `ChipGroup` (radiogroup arrow keys), `ResetViewButton`, look in `src/lib/styles/chip.css` ([UXD-014](../../../docs/uxs/decisions/UXD-014-one-chip-and-filter.md)); a component never writes its own chip rules.
- **Surface HUD** (used by `SurfaceScene`) — `ViewToggleButton`, `View3dControls`, `LayerChipRow` (the one flat layer-chip row, also used by Explore; UXD-015), `TierContextCard`, `TacticalScan`, `LensLegend`, `Panorama*`. **Stations** — `StationBlueprint`, `StationAssemblyControl`, `StationTimelineStrip`, `StationOrbitBanner`.
- **Look-alikes** — `EpochTimelineStrip` (/missions, /fleet) vs `TimelineNavigator` (/missions year window); `MediaPlayer` (modal) vs `VideoThumb` (resting tile).
- **Debug** — `DebugPanel` (`?debug=1`) shows page tabs registered via `DebugPanelRegistrar` / `debug-panel-context.ts`; also `RenderingDebugRegistrar`, `FlybyDebugViewer`, `AscentCameraDebug`.
- **Attribution/links** — `ImageCredit`, `LinkCredit`, `LearnLink` (ADR-051 rules).
- **Accent colour** — the accent tokens (`--color-accent`, `-hover`, `-soft`, `-edge`, `-strong`, `--color-state`, `--color-focus`, `--color-ok`) live in `src/lib/styles/tokens.css`; no component writes its own accent colour (UXD-001, A3); the chip active look is defined once in `chip.css`.
- **SEO** — `OuterEntityPage` (the template of `/explore/galaxies/<id>` and `/explore/structures/<id>`: lede, bio, rows, Related list, learn-more, the link into the scene; ADR-096 item 18), `JsonLd` (schema.org data block in the head; build the data with `$lib/json-ld`), `SiteEntityPage` (the /moon|mars|venus/[id] entity page), `EntityIndex` (the A–Z list at the foot of /missions and /fleet; the scene routes link their entity pages from the INDEX panel instead, `SurfaceIndexPanel` and `ScaleIndex`, whose rows link `/explore/<id>`, `/explore/stars/<id>` and `/explore/black-holes/<id>`, ADR-096 items 11, 13 and 16) and `entity-page.css` (the content layout of every entity page: missions, fleet, sites, bodies, Lab formulas; ADR-096; container, title, lead and labels come from the page shell). **Page shell** — `src/lib/styles/page-shell.css` (loaded by `app.css`): `.page-shell`, `.page-crumb`, `.page-title`, `.page-lead`, `.page-section-label`, `.page-section-title` for every text page ([UXD-012](../../../docs/uxs/decisions/UXD-012-one-page-shell.md)).

Pointers: TA.md §components · AGENTS.md (i18n, mobile-first). No doc states why panels share one shell; the `Panel.svelte` prop comments are the only record.
